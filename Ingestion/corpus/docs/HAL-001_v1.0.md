# FDA Letter on a Post-Approval Commitment: Velorastat Film-Coated Tablets (NDA 980142)

Department of Health and Human Services, Food and Drug Administration, Center for Drug Evaluation and Research, Office of Pharmaceutical Quality, 10903 New Hampshire Avenue, Silver Spring, MD 20993

October 6, 2025

NDA 980142. FDA Reference ID: 5531907. Post-Marketing Commitment Tracking No. PMC 4127-2.

Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals, Inc., Attention: Dr. Helena Marsh, Director of Regulatory Affairs CMC, 400 Crestline Parkway, Suite 210, Princeton, NJ 08540

Dear Dr. Marsh:

We refer to your New Drug Application (NDA) 980142 for Velorastat film-coated tablets, 25 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg, packaged in high-density polyethylene (HDPE) bottles with desiccant. The application was submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act on June 14, 2022, and was approved on April 12, 2023. At approval, the Agency granted a 24-month expiration dating period. That period was based on long-term and accelerated stability data from the primary registration batches manufactured at your Raleigh, North Carolina facility.

During review, the Agency noted that no long-term data were yet available for batches made at commercial scale with the final commercial equipment train. The Agency and Aldencourt discussed this at the late-cycle meeting held on February 21, 2023. Aldencourt then confirmed its agreement in written correspondence dated March 3, 2023, submitted as eCTD Sequence 0014. The resulting post-marketing commitment was listed in the approval letter. Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals committed to submit 36-month long-term stability data for the first three commercial batches of Velorastat film-coated tablets.

Under this commitment, the first three commercial batches must be placed on long-term storage at 25°C ± 2°C and 60% ± 5% relative humidity. Testing must follow the stability protocol in the approved application, and interim results are to be reported in the NDA annual reports. Your most recent annual report was submitted on May 30, 2025, as eCTD Sequence 0031. It contained 24-month long-term results for all three batches across all strengths. The Agency's review of that report found no out-of-specification results and no adverse trends in assay, degradation products, dissolution or water content.

The final report for this commitment is due on March 31, 2026. As of the date of this letter, this commitment remains open. The commitment is not past due. In the Agency's tracking system its status is recorded as "pending," and this letter does not identify a deficiency. We are writing to remind you of the upcoming milestone and to set out what the final submission should contain.

<!-- page 2 -->

The final report should include the following:

- Complete 36-month long-term results for each of the three commercial batches, together with all earlier time points.
- Batch numbers, manufacturing dates, batch sizes and the bottle configurations tested.
- A statistical evaluation of the data under ICH Q1E, including any analysis supporting a proposed extension of the expiration dating period.
- A discussion of any trends, investigations or atypical results seen during the study.

If you intend to extend the expiration dating period on the basis of these data, please follow the reporting category specified in the approved stability protocol. If you have questions about the appropriate category, contact us before submission. Please identify the submission clearly in the cover letter as a response to Post-Marketing Commitment PMC 4127-2.

If Aldencourt expects that it cannot meet the due date, please notify the Agency in writing before March 31, 2026. The notification should explain the reason for the delay and propose a revised timeline. The Agency will then consider the request and respond. Under 21 CFR 314.81(b)(2)(vii), the status of this commitment must also continue to be reported in each annual report until it is fulfilled.

Dr. Helena Marsh, Director of Regulatory Affairs CMC at Aldencourt, is the responsible contact for this commitment. Please inform us promptly if this responsibility moves to another person. Your contact at the Agency is Ms. Danielle Ortiz, Regulatory Project Manager, Office of Pharmaceutical Quality. You may reach her by telephone at (301) 796-4482 or by email at danielle.ortiz@fda.hhs.gov.

We appreciate Aldencourt's cooperation to date and look forward to receiving the final stability report.

Sincerely,

Michael T. Brennan, Ph.D., Director, Division of Post-Marketing Activities I, Office of Lifecycle Drug Products, Office of Pharmaceutical Quality, Center for Drug Evaluation and Research, U.S. Food and Drug Administration

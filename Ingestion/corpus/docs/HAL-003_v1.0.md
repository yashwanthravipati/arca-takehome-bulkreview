# FDA Acknowledgement of a Fulfilled Commitment: Calvidronate Concentrate for Infusion (NDA 980377)

Department of Health and Human Services, Food and Drug Administration, Center for Drug Evaluation and Research, 10903 New Hampshire Avenue, Silver Spring, MD 20993

December 9, 2025

NDA 980377, Postmarketing Commitment 4721-3, Supplement Sequence 0058, FDA Reference CDER-OPQ-2025-11873

Dr. Priya Raman, Associate Director of Regulatory Affairs, Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals, 1400 Meridian Park Drive, Princeton, NJ 08540

Dear Dr. Raman:

This letter refers to your New Drug Application (NDA) 980377 for Calvidronate concentrate for solution for infusion, 5 mg/mL, submitted under section 505(b) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act. The product is supplied in 4 mL, 10 mL and 20 mL Type I glass vials and is labeled for refrigerated storage. We also refer to your submission dated November 21, 2025 (Sequence 0058), which contained the final report of the photostability study described below.

During the review of the original application, the Agency noted that the photostability data in the submission were generated mainly with the 4 mL and 10 mL presentations. The 20 mL vial has a larger fill volume and a different carton configuration, and the Agency considered that the data did not fully show that the carton gives the 20 mL vial adequate protection from light. We discussed this question with your representatives during a teleconference on January 23, 2024 (Meeting ID 0442-T). Aldencourt then agreed to generate confirmatory data after approval rather than delay action on the application. The commitment was recorded in the approval letter for NDA 980377 dated March 14, 2024.

Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals committed to conduct a confirmatory photostability study of the 20 mL Calvidronate vial in its carton. The study report was due on November 30, 2025. Your submission of November 21, 2025 was received before this date.

The submitted report describes a study conducted according to the principles of ICH Q1B, Option 2. Filled 20 mL vials from three commercial-scale batches were tested in three configurations: in the marketed carton, as directly exposed vials, and as dark controls wrapped in aluminum foil. Samples received an overall illumination of not less than 1.2 million lux hours and an integrated near-ultraviolet energy of not less than 200 watt hours per square meter. Vials stored in the carton showed no meaningful change compared with dark controls in assay, related substances, pH, color, clarity or particulate matter, and all results stayed within the approved specification. Directly exposed vials showed a measurable increase in total related substances. This confirms that the drug product is sensitive to light and that the carton protects it.

<!-- page 2 -->

The Agency's Office of Pharmaceutical Quality has reviewed the report and agrees with your conclusions. The data support the current storage statement directing users to keep vials in the original carton to protect them from light until the time of use, and they support the current expiration dating for the 20 mL presentation. No changes to labeling or to the approved specification are needed. The Agency considers this commitment fulfilled.

No further submissions are needed for this commitment, and it will be shown as fulfilled in the Agency's postmarketing commitment records. You must still meet all other postmarketing requirements and commitments for NDA 980377, as well as the reporting obligations under 21 CFR 314.81. If future changes to the container closure system, carton or secondary packaging of any Calvidronate presentation could affect light protection, please assess them under 21 CFR 314.70 and support them with suitable photostability data.

Dr. Priya Raman, Associate Director of Regulatory Affairs, is the responsible contact for this commitment. Please direct any questions about this letter or about NDA 980377 to Ms. Danielle Okafor, Regulatory Project Manager, at 301-796-4418 or by secure email through the CDER electronic correspondence system. Please include the application number and FDA reference number shown above in any related correspondence.

We appreciate the timely completion of this study and the quality of the information submitted.

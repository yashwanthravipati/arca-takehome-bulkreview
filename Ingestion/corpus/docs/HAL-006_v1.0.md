# FDA Acknowledgement of a Fulfilled Commitment: Zentoprazine Site Transfer (NDA 980563)

Department of Health and Human Services, Food and Drug Administration, Center for Drug Evaluation and Research, Office of Pharmaceutical Quality, 10903 New Hampshire Avenue, Silver Spring, MD 20993-0002

Date: October 20, 2025

Our reference: NDA 980563 / S-021, OPQ Correspondence No. CMC-2025-07418

Your reference: Supplement amendment, eCTD Sequence 0134, received September 18, 2025

Sofia Brandt, Site Regulatory Lead, Lindgren Site, Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals, 4400 Harborview Parkway, Wilmington, DE 19801

Dear Ms. Brandt:

Please refer to your New Drug Application (NDA) 980563 for Zentoprazine extended-release tablets, 50 mg, 100 mg and 200 mg, packaged in high-density polyethylene (HDPE) bottles. Please also refer to your prior approval supplement S-021, submitted under section 506A of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act on August 2, 2024. That supplement proposed transferring commercial manufacture, packaging and release testing of the drug product to the Lindgren site.

During review of the supplement, the Agency held a Type A teleconference with Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals on November 14, 2024 (Meeting ID 2024-CMC-3391). At that meeting we noted that the supplement contained only limited real-time stability data for batches manufactured at the Lindgren site. Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals agreed to supplement those data after approval. The Agency approved S-021 on January 29, 2025, and the approval letter recorded this agreement as a post-approval chemistry, manufacturing and controls commitment.

Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals committed to provide comparability stability data for Zentoprazine extended-release tablets manufactured at the Lindgren site. The comparability data were due on September 30, 2025. We received your submission on September 18, 2025, before the agreed date.

The submission contains the following data:

- 12 months of long-term stability data at 25°C/60% relative humidity.
- 6 months of accelerated stability data at 40°C/75% relative humidity.
- Data on three consecutive commercial-scale batches of each strength manufactured at the Lindgren site, packaged in the approved HDPE bottle configuration.
- Side-by-side comparisons with historical batches from the previous manufacturing site, covering assay, degradation products, multipoint dissolution, water content and microbial limits.
- A report describing the statistical evaluation of the dissolution profiles, including similarity factor (f2) calculations at each time point.
- A trend analysis of total impurities across both sites.

Our Office of Pharmaceutical Quality has reviewed this information. All results at both storage conditions remained within the approved specifications. Dissolution profiles for batches from the Lindgren site were similar to those of batches from the previous site at all strengths, with f2 values above 50. We found no new degradation products and no adverse trends. The data support the conclusion that the site transfer did not adversely affect the stability profile of the product. The data also continue to support the currently approved expiration dating period of 24 months when the product is stored at controlled room temperature. The Agency considers this commitment fulfilled. We have updated our records to show the commitment as closed, and no further submission is needed to satisfy it.

<!-- page 2 -->

Please continue the ongoing stability studies on the batches described above through the full proposed expiration period. Report the results in your NDA annual reports as described in 21 CFR 314.81(b)(2). If any result falls outside specification or shows an unexpected trend, report it promptly through the appropriate regulatory mechanism, including a Field Alert Report where 21 CFR 314.81(b)(1) requires one. Any future changes to the manufacturing process, equipment, container closure system or specifications at the Lindgren site must be reported under 21 CFR 314.70.

Sofia Brandt, Site Regulatory Lead at the Lindgren site, is the responsible contact for this commitment. Please make sure that future correspondence about the stability program for this product cites NDA 980563 and the correspondence number shown above.

This letter confirms the status of a post-authorization quality commitment for Zentoprazine extended-release tablets, 50 mg, 100 mg and 200 mg, packaged in high-density polyethylene (HDPE) bottles. The commitment was made during the review of the New Drug Submission (control number 271944), which Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals filed on 14 April 2025.

During the quality review, the Bureau of Pharmaceutical Sciences examined the drug product specification and the supporting long-term and accelerated stability data. The proposed acceptance criterion for the degradation product ZTP N-oxide was set mainly on the basis of early development batches. The criterion was wider than the levels seen in the primary stability batches and in the process validation batches made at the commercial site. In a Clarification Request issued on 9 December 2025, the Bureau asked the company to justify the proposed limit or to tighten it in line with the commercial manufacturing experience.

Aldencourt's response of 22 January 2026 explained that the company did not yet have enough long-term data on commercial-scale batches to propose a statistically justified tighter limit. It proposed keeping the current criterion for the time being and submitting a revised specification once additional stability results were available. The Bureau and the company discussed this approach at a teleconference on 4 February 2026 (meeting reference TC-2026-0148). The company confirmed the commitment in writing on 11 February 2026. The Bureau found the interim limit acceptable for the protection of patients, given the toxicological qualification data provided, and a Notice of Compliance was issued on 17 March 2026 with the commitment recorded on file.

Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals committed to submit a revised drug product specification for Zentoprazine extended-release tablets with a tightened limit for ZTP N-oxide. The revised specification is due on 31 January 2027. This commitment is open and is not yet due. The Bureau has not received a revised specification to date. No action is required beyond meeting the agreed timeline, and this letter does not indicate any concern with the company's progress.

The submission fulfilling this commitment should be filed as a post-Notice of Compliance change in line with the applicable guidance on post-NOC changes for quality. It should include:

<!-- page 2 -->

- the revised drug product specification;
- a justification for the tightened ZTP N-oxide acceptance criterion based on batch analysis and long-term stability data from commercial-scale batches of all three strengths;
- confirmation that the analytical procedure remains validated across the revised range;
- any consequential updates to the certificate of analysis template and the stability protocol.

Please reference control number 271944 and the correspondence reference above in the cover letter.

If Aldencourt expects that the revised specification cannot be submitted by the agreed date, please notify the Bureau in writing as early as possible, and no later than 30 November 2026. The notice should give the reason for the delay and a proposed new timeline. Similarly, if interim stability results show ZTP N-oxide trending towards the current acceptance criterion in any marketed batch, please inform the Bureau promptly, in addition to meeting your obligations under the Food and Drug Regulations.

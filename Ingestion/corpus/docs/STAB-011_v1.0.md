# STAB-011 Stability Protocol for Moxilurine Capsules Filled into HPMC Shells from an Alternate Supplier

**Document number:** STAB-011
**Version:** 1.0
**Effective date:** 2025-04-14
**Status:** Current
**Product:** Product D, Moxilurine 150 mg hard capsules (HPMC shells)
**Document type:** Stability protocol
**Owning department:** Regulatory Affairs CMC, Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals, Linfield Site

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This protocol defines the stability study that supports the introduction of hydroxypropyl methylcellulose (HPMC) hard capsule shells from an alternate supplier for Moxilurine 150 mg hard capsules (Product D). Moxilurine capsules are currently filled into HPMC shells from the approved primary supplier, Halvern Capsule Systems.

1.2 The study generates long-term and accelerated stability data on capsules filled into shells from Corvane Capsule Technologies. The data show whether the change in shell supplier affects the chemical stability, dissolution performance or physical integrity of the drug product. The data will support the post-approval variation submission under change control CC-2025-0031.

1.3 The study is designed according to ICH Q1A(R2). It is evaluated according to ICH Q1E, using the registration stability data generated under STAB-010 as the comparator.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This protocol applies to Moxilurine 150 mg hard capsules filled into size 1 opaque white HPMC shells (Corvane shell code CV-H1-W02). The capsules are manufactured at the Aldencourt Linfield Site. They are packaged in PVC/PVDC blisters with push-through aluminium lidding foil.

2.2 Product D is registered in PVC/PVDC blisters and in 30-, 60- and 90-count HDPE bottles with induction-sealed polypropylene closures. This protocol covers the blister presentation only. The PVC/PVDC blister is the least moisture-protective primary pack registered for Product D. HPMC shell behaviour under high humidity is the main technical concern for this change, so the blister was chosen as the pack for this study. The HDPE bottle presentations are outside the scope of STAB-011. These presentations remain covered by the registration data in STAB-010 and the ongoing programme in STAB-012.

2.3 This protocol does not change the commercial shelf life, storage statement or release specification of Product D. These remain as defined in SPEC-D and in the marketing authorisation.

## 3. Responsibilities

3.1 **Regulatory Affairs CMC** owns this protocol. It ensures alignment with the registered dossier and compiles the stability data for the variation submission.

3.2 **Stability Coordination (Quality Control)** is responsible for:
- placing and pulling samples;
- maintaining the stability chambers and their monitoring records;
- issuing pull lists according to SOP-QC-0412, Stability Sample Management;
- maintaining the stability data summary for each batch.

3.3 **QC Analytical Laboratory** performs testing according to the analytical methods listed in Section 5. It also raises laboratory investigations and deviations according to SOP-QA-0207, Deviation Management, and SOP-QC-0318, Out-of-Specification Investigations.

3.4 **Quality Assurance** is responsible for:
- approving this protocol and any amendments;
- reviewing and closing deviations;
- approving the final stability report;
- confirming that results are evaluated against the acceptance criteria in Section 8.

3.5 **Production (Encapsulation and Packaging)** manufactures and packages the stability batches according to the approved master batch records. It provides executed batch records to Stability Coordination before sample placement.

3.6 **Supply Chain Quality** maintains the supplier qualification file for Corvane Capsule Technologies. It provides certificates of analysis for each shell lot used.

## 4. Materials and Batches

4.1 Three batches of Moxilurine 150 mg hard capsules were manufactured at commercial scale (180,000 capsules per batch). Each batch was filled into alternate-supplier HPMC shells. All three batches used the same drug substance lot and excipient lots as far as practicable, so that the shell supplier is the only material variable compared with the STAB-010 registration batches.

| D-25-0108 | 2025-02-19 | MXL-API-24-0441 | CV-2412-0891 | PVC/PVDC (250 µm PVC / 60 g/m² PVDC), 10 capsules per blister card | 2025-02-25 |

4.2 Each blister card is packed in its commercial carton (3 cards per carton, 30 capsules). Samples are stored in the carton during the study.

4.3 Initial (T0) testing was completed on all three batches between 2025-02-27 and 2025-03-03. Samples were placed into the stability chambers on 2025-03-03. Study initiation was authorised under CC-2025-0031 on the basis of draft version 0.2 of this protocol (see Section 12).

4.4 Retained reserve samples (two cartons per batch per condition) are stored in each chamber. They are used for confirmatory or investigational testing according to SOP-QC-0412.

## 5. Analytical Methods

5.1 All testing uses the validated methods listed in Table 2. These are the same methods used for release testing under SPEC-D and for the registration study under STAB-010. Before use with alternate-supplier shells, the methods were verified for specificity against Corvane shell placebo (verification report VR-AM-25-006).

<!-- page 3 -->

**Table 2. Analytical methods**

| Test | Method number | Technique | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Appearance (contents and shell) | AM-D-103 | Visual inspection | Includes shell brittleness and deformation assessment, see 5.3 |
| Assay | AM-D-101 | Reversed-phase HPLC, UV 254 nm | Same chromatographic run as related substances |
| Related substances (MXL-dimer, unspecified, total) | AM-D-101 | Reversed-phase HPLC, UV 254 nm | Reporting threshold 0.05% |
| Dissolution | AM-D-102 | USP Apparatus 1 (baskets), 100 rpm, 900 mL pH 6.8 phosphate buffer, 37.0 °C ± 0.5 °C | HPLC finish; single point at 45 minutes |
| Water content (capsule fill) | AM-D-104 | Coulometric Karl Fischer | Reported for information |
| Shell loss on drying | AM-D-105 | Gravimetric, 105 °C, 2 hours | Reported for information |

5.2 Dissolution testing is performed on calibrated dissolution apparatus qualified under SOP-QC-0221, Dissolution Apparatus Qualification and Performance Verification. A vessel temperature check is recorded before each run. A failed check invalidates the run.

5.3 The shell integrity assessment under AM-D-103 examines 20 capsules per batch per timepoint. Each capsule is inspected for:
- cracking;
- splitting at the cap/body junction;
- brittleness, assessed by a standardised finger-pressure test;
- softening;
- dimpling;
- other deformation.

## 6. Storage Conditions

6.1 STAB-010 placed the registration batches at 25°C/60% RH, 30°C/65% RH, 30°C/75% RH and 40°C/75% RH to support Climatic Zones I to IVb. This study uses only the most humid long-term condition and the accelerated condition. These two conditions are the most discriminating for HPMC shell performance and for MXL-dimer formation, which is humidity-sensitive.

6.2 Capsules filled into the alternate-supplier HPMC shells will be stored at 30°C ± 2°C / 75% RH ± 5% RH and at 40°C ± 2°C / 75% RH ± 5% RH in PVC/PVDC blisters. The long-term condition uses chamber SC-07 and the accelerated condition uses chamber SC-09. Both chambers are mapped and qualified according to SOP-ENG-0115, Stability Chamber Qualification. Both are continuously monitored by the site environmental monitoring system, with alarm limits set inside the stated tolerances.

**Table 3. Storage conditions and chamber assignment**

| Study arm | Condition | Chamber ID | Backup chamber | Orientation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Long-term | 30 °C ± 2 °C / 75% RH ± 5% RH | SC-07 | SC-08 | Cartons upright |
| Accelerated | 40 °C ± 2 °C / 75% RH ± 5% RH | SC-09 | SC-10 | Cartons upright |

6.3 Excursions outside the stated tolerances are assessed according to SOP-QC-0419, Stability Chamber Excursions. Any excursion longer than 24 hours, or any excursion that may have affected sample integrity, is handled as a deviation according to Section 9.

6.4 Photostability testing according to ICH Q1B is not repeated in this study. The Corvane shell is opacified with titanium dioxide at a level equivalent to the current shell (supplier certificate and incoming test data, shell lot CoA file SQ-CV-2025-01). The registration Q1B data in STAB-010 showed that the drug product is not photosensitive in the opaque shell.

## 7. Testing Schedule

7.1 Samples will be tested at 0, 1, 3 and 6 months under accelerated storage and at 0, 3, 6, 9 and 12 months under long-term storage. The T0 results are common to both arms. Pulls are made within ±7 days of the nominal date for timepoints up to and including 6 months, and within ±14 days for later timepoints. Testing is started within 14 days of the pull date.

7.2 Each long-term and accelerated pull uses four cartons per batch. For each batch, 24 cartons were placed in SC-07 and 16 cartons in SC-09. This quantity covers the scheduled pulls and the reserve samples described in 4.4. It also allows the long-term arm to be extended without re-placement if Regulatory Affairs decides to continue it.

7.3 If a significant change, as defined in ICH Q1A(R2), occurs at the accelerated condition, the long-term data will be evaluated according to ICH Q1E. Regulatory Affairs will consult QA on whether an intermediate condition study is needed. No intermediate condition is scheduled in this version of the protocol.

## 8. Acceptance Criteria

8.1 Acceptance criteria for this study are assay 95.0–105.0% of label claim, MXL-dimer not more than 0.2%, total impurities not more than 1.0%, dissolution not less than 75% (Q) in 45 minutes, and no capsule shell brittleness or deformation on visual inspection. The assay and impurity limits are consistent with the shelf-life limits in SPEC-D. The dissolution criterion is applied using the USP <711> acceptance table for Stages 1 to 3.

**Table 6. Tests reported without acceptance criteria**

| Test | Reporting requirement |
|---|---|
| Unspecified impurities | Report each impurity at or above 0.05% and its relative retention time; trend against STAB-010 |
| Water content (fill) | Report result (% w/w); trend against T0 |
| Shell loss on drying | Report result (% w/w); compare with Corvane CoA range |
| Appearance of capsule contents | Describe |

8.2 Results will be trended and compared with the corresponding STAB-010 timepoints for the registration batches in the current Halvern shells. Shelf-life support will be assessed according to ICH Q1E. A statistically significant difference in degradation rate, or in the dissolution trend, between alternate-supplier and current-supplier batches will be reported in the stability summary. This applies even when all results remain within the acceptance criteria.

8.3 Any result outside the acceptance criteria is investigated according to SOP-QC-0318. Out-of-trend results are evaluated according to SOP-QC-0322, Stability Trending and Out-of-Trend Evaluation.

## 9. Deviation Handling

9.1 Deviations from this protocol include:
- missed or late pulls;
- chamber excursions;
- equipment failures that affect testing;
- use of an unapproved method version;
- testing outside the window in 7.1.

All such deviations are recorded and investigated according to SOP-QA-0207. Each deviation is assessed for impact on data validity and on the conclusions of the study. QA approves the closure of each deviation.

<!-- page 6 -->

9.2 Deviations raised during the study are listed in the stability report. Deviations raised before the effective date of this version are recorded below.

9.3 Deviation DEV-2025-0158 was raised when dissolution apparatus DS-03 failed its vessel temperature check at the 1-month accelerated timepoint, and the test was repeated on apparatus DS-02 with passing results. The failed check occurred before any sample was introduced. No results were generated on DS-03, so no data were invalidated or excluded.

9.4 The repeat run on DS-02 was performed within the testing window defined in 7.1. It used capsules from the same 1-month accelerated pull. DS-02 had passed its vessel temperature check and was within its performance verification interval. Root cause analysis attributed the failure on DS-03 to a faulty heater circulator. The circulator was replaced, and DS-03 was requalified according to SOP-QC-0221 before it was returned to service. QA closed DEV-2025-0158 with no impact on study validity.

## 10. Post-Approval Commitments

10.1 Upon approval, the first two commercial batches filled into the alternate-supplier shells will be placed on long-term stability through 36 months. These batches will be stored at the long-term condition in Section 6 and tested at the timepoints defined in STAB-012. The acceptance criteria will be those of SPEC-D in force at the time of testing.

10.2 Once the commitment batches have been placed, the alternate-supplier shell presentation will be included in the annual ongoing stability programme under STAB-012. Regulatory Affairs CMC will track the commitment in the post-approval commitment register (RA-PAC-D-004). The 36-month data will be reported to the competent authorities as required by the approval conditions.

10.3 The long-term arm of this study may be extended beyond 12 months using the samples described in 7.2. Any extension will be documented in an amendment to this protocol.

# Registration Stability Protocol for Zentoprazine Extended-Release Tablets 50 mg, 100 mg and 200 mg

**Document number:** STAB-013
**Version:** 2.0
**Effective date:** 2024-04-08
**Status:** Current
**Product:** Product E, Zentoprazine Extended-Release Tablets 50 mg, 100 mg and 200 mg, in HDPE bottles
**Owner:** Regulatory Affairs, CMC Stability

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This protocol defines the registration stability study for Zentoprazine Extended-Release Tablets 50 mg, 100 mg and 200 mg (Product E). The study will generate the data needed to support the proposed shelf life and storage statement in the marketing authorisation application.

1.2 The protocol establishes the following elements of the study:

- batches;
- container closure system;
- storage conditions;
- pull points;
- analytical methods;
- acceptance criteria;
- reduced-design approach;
- post-approval commitments.

These elements are designed in accordance with ICH Q1A(R2), Q1B, Q1D and Q1E.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This protocol applies to the primary registration batches of Zentoprazine Extended-Release Tablets. These batches were manufactured at the Aldencourt Fairhaven manufacturing site and packaged in the proposed commercial container closure system.

2.2 Other stability studies for this product are covered by separate documents:

- Stability studies supporting manufacture at the Lindgren site are covered by STAB-014.
- The ongoing (commitment and annual) stability program, including its matrixing design, is covered by STAB-015.

2.3 This protocol does not cover drug substance stability, in-use stability or bulk hold-time studies. Bulk hold-time studies are addressed under SOP-PRD-0218.

## 3. Responsibilities

- Compiles stability data for submission and for the annual report.

3.2 **QC Stability Group**

3.4 **Quality Assurance**

## 4. Materials and batches

4.1 **Product composition.** Zentoprazine Extended-Release Tablets are hydrophilic matrix tablets. All three strengths are compressed from a common matrix granulation (granulation code ZTP-GR-01), which contains hypromellose K100M as the release-controlling polymer. The strengths differ only in tablet weight and debossing:

| Strength | Tablet weight (mg) | Debossing | Film-coat colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50 mg | 200 | "ZT" / "50" | White |
| 100 mg | 400 | "ZT" / "100" | White |
| 200 mg | 800 | "ZT" / "200" | White |

4.2 **Container closure system.** Tablets are packaged as follows:

- 30-count white HDPE bottles (60 cc for the 50 mg strength; 100 cc for the 200 mg strength);
- 33 mm child-resistant polypropylene closures with induction-sealed foil liners;
- a 1 g silica gel desiccant canister in each bottle.

This is the proposed commercial presentation.

4.3 **Registration batches.** Three batches of each bracketing strength were manufactured at pilot scale. Pilot scale is at least one tenth of the proposed commercial batch size of 600,000 tablets for 50 mg and 150,000 tablets for 200 mg. Each batch used a different lot of drug substance.

## 5. Analytical methods

5.1 All testing is performed with methods validated per ICH Q2(R2) and described in SPEC-E. The method list is provided in Appendix B.

5.2 **Assay and related substances** are determined by a single stability-indicating reversed-phase HPLC method, AM-ZTP-001.
- The method resolves zentoprazine from ZTP N-oxide (the principal oxidative degradant), from ZTP desmethyl (a process impurity) and from unspecified degradants.
- The reporting threshold is 0.05%.

5.3 **Drug release** is determined by AM-ZTP-002 using the following conditions:
- USP Apparatus 1 (baskets) at 100 rpm;
- 900 mL of pH 6.8 phosphate buffer at 37.0°C ± 0.5°C;
- UV quantitation at 274 nm;
- sampling at 2, 8 and 16 hours.

<!-- page 3 -->

5.4 **Other tests:**
- Water content by Karl Fischer coulometric titration (AM-ZTP-003);
- Appearance (AM-ZTP-004);
- Microbial enumeration (AM-GEN-021).

Microbial enumeration is performed at selected long-term time points only.

## 6. Storage conditions

6.1 Samples will be stored at 25°C ± 2°C / 60% RH ± 5% RH (long-term), 30°C ± 2°C / 65% RH ± 5% RH (intermediate) and 40°C ± 2°C / 75% RH ± 5% RH (accelerated). Bottles are stored upright and in closed configuration.

6.2 **Chamber assignments.** Chambers are qualified and continuously monitored per SOP-QC-0340. Excursions are handled per Section 9.

| Condition | Chamber ID | Location | EMS probe ID |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25°C / 60% RH | CH-07 | Building 4, Room 4.112 | EMS-0703 |
| 30°C / 65% RH | CH-09 | Building 4, Room 4.112 | EMS-0905 |
| 40°C / 75% RH | CH-11 | Building 4, Room 4.115 | EMS-1102 |

6.3 **Photostability.** Photostability testing is performed once on one batch of each bracketing strength (ZT23R001 and ZT23R004) per ICH Q1B, Option 2:
- Exposure is not less than 1.2 million lux hours and not less than 200 W·h/m² near-UV energy.
- Tablets are exposed both directly and in the immediate pack.
- A dark control wrapped in aluminium foil is included.

6.4 **Supplementary studies.** A freeze-thaw cycling study (−20°C / 25°C, three cycles) and a 50°C / ambient humidity stress study are performed as supporting studies under SOP-QC-0366. They are not part of the registration dataset.

## 7. Testing schedule

7.1 Long-term testing will occur at 0, 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, 24 and 36 months, intermediate testing at 0, 6, 9 and 12 months, and accelerated testing at 0, 3 and 6 months. The initial (time zero) results are generated once per batch and serve as the starting point for all three conditions.

7.2 **Pull windows.** Pull windows are calculated from the date samples are placed in the chamber:
- ±7 days for time points up to and including 12 months;
- ±14 days for time points after 12 months.

Testing should begin within 14 days of the pull. Pulled samples awaiting testing are held at 2–8°C.

7.3 **Test matrix.** The tests performed at each time point are summarised below. X = test performed; — = not performed.

| Test | Initial | LT 3–36 M | LT 12, 24, 36 M | INT 6–12 M | ACC 3, 6 M |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Appearance | X | X | X | X | X |
| Assay | X | X | X | X | X |
| Related substances | X | X | X | X | X |
| Drug release | X | X | X | X | X |
| Water content | X | X | X | X | X |
| Microbial enumeration | X | — | X | — | — |

7.4 **Intermediate condition.** Intermediate samples are tested according to the schedule above regardless of the accelerated outcome. This supports evaluation under ICH Q1A(R2) should a significant change occur at the accelerated condition.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

8.1 All strengths must comply with assay 95.0–105.0% of label claim, ZTP N-oxide not more than 0.3%, total impurities not more than 1.2%, and drug release of 15–35% at 2 hours, 50–75% at 8 hours and not less than 85% at 16 hours. These criteria are identical to the shelf-life limits in SPEC-E.

8.2 **Release limits.** Release limits applied at batch certification (for example, assay 97.0–103.0% of label claim) are tighter than the shelf-life limits. They are not applied to stability samples.

| Test | Shelf-life acceptance criterion |
|---|---|
| Appearance | White, capsule-shaped, film-coated tablet with strength-specific debossing |
| Assay | 95.0–105.0% of label claim |
| ZTP N-oxide | NMT 0.3% |
| ZTP desmethyl | NMT 0.2% |
| Any unspecified impurity | NMT 0.10% |
| Total impurities | NMT 1.2% |
| Drug release, 2 h | 15–35% |
| Drug release, 8 h | 50–75% |
| Drug release, 16 h | NLT 85% |
| Water content | NMT 3.0% w/w |
| TAMC / TYMC | NMT 1000 CFU/g / NMT 100 CFU/g |

8.3 **Drug release evaluation.** Drug release results are evaluated against USP <711> acceptance table 2 (levels L1 to L3).

8.4 **Data evaluation.** Stability data are evaluated per ICH Q1E. Assay and ZTP N-oxide are evaluated by regression analysis with 95% one-sided confidence limits. Batch poolability is tested at the 0.25 significance level.

## 9. Deviation handling

9.1 Any departure from this protocol must be documented as a deviation in the electronic quality management system per SOP-QA-0112. Examples of such departures include:
- missed or out-of-window pulls;
- chamber excursions;
- sample mix-ups;
- testing outside the defined timeframe;
- use of an unapproved method version.

9.2 **Notification.** The QC Stability Group notifies QA within one business day of discovery. QA assigns a classification (minor, major or critical) and an owner.

9.3 **Impact assessment.** The assessment considers the following:
- the magnitude and duration of the departure;
- the stability-indicating attributes affected;
- the available trend data;
- whether the data remain valid for regulatory use.

Chamber excursions are assessed against EMS records and mean kinetic temperature calculations per SOP-QC-0347.

9.4 **Closure.** Corrective and preventive actions are tracked per SOP-QA-0120. Deviations must be closed before the affected data are included in a stability report. All deviations associated with this study are listed in Appendix C.

## 10. Bracketing and matrixing

10.1 Bracketing is applied by testing only the 50 mg and 200 mg strengths, because the three strengths share a common matrix granulation and differ only in tablet weight and debossing.

10.2 **Basis for the extremes.** The 50 mg and 200 mg strengths represent the extremes of tablet weight. They also represent the extremes of tablet surface-area-to-volume ratio, which is the parameter most relevant to release from a hydrophilic matrix.

10.3 **Container closure.** All strengths use the same HDPE bottle resin, closure and desiccant. The 100 mg strength is packaged in the 60 cc bottle used for the 50 mg strength. The headspace-to-tablet-mass ratio of the 100 mg presentation therefore lies between those of the two tested presentations.

10.4 **Matrixing.** No matrixing is applied in this registration study; all batches are tested at all time points. A matrixing design for the ongoing program is defined separately in STAB-015.

10.5 **Use of results.** The shelf life assigned to the 100 mg strength will not exceed the shorter of the shelf lives supported by the 50 mg and 200 mg data, per ICH Q1D.

## 11. Post-approval commitments

11.1 The applicant commits to placing the first three production batches of the 50 mg and 200 mg strengths on long-term stability and to reporting 36-month data in the annual report.

11.2 **Commitment batches.** These batches will be tested at the long-term condition defined in Section 6 using the methods and acceptance criteria of this protocol. Their sampling will follow the long-term sampling plan in Appendix A.

11.3 **Annual batches.** After the commitment batches, one production batch per strength per year will be placed on the ongoing program under STAB-015.

11.4 **Lindgren site.** Batches manufactured at the Lindgren site are covered by STAB-014 and are not part of this commitment.

## Appendix A: Sampling plan

A.1 **Bottles per pull.** For each batch and pull, the following bottles are removed:
- two test bottles of 30 tablets each;
- one reserve bottle.

At time points that include microbial enumeration, one additional bottle is pulled.

A.2 **Tablet use per pull:**
- Assay: 10 tablets (composite);
- Related substances: 10 tablets (composite);
- Drug release: 12 tablets (L1/L2);
- Water content: 5 tablets;
- Appearance: 10 tablets (non-destructive, reused for assay).

| Condition | Pull points (months) | Bottles per pull | Extra bottles (micro) | Bottles per batch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Initial | 0 | 3 | 1 | 4 |
| 25°C / 60% RH | 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, 24, 36 | 3 | 3 | 24 |
| 30°C / 65% RH | 6, 9, 12 | 3 | 0 | 9 |
| 40°C / 75% RH | 3, 6 | 3 | 0 | 6 |
| Photostability (ZT23R001, ZT23R004 only) | Single exposure | 4 | 0 | 4 |
| Contingency reserve | — | — | — | 6 |

A.3 **Totals.** In total, 49 bottles are placed per batch, and 53 bottles for ZT23R001 and ZT23R004, which include photostability. Across the six registration batches, 302 bottles are placed.

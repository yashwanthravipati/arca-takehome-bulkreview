This letter concerns a post-authorisation measure (PAM) for Tirexafen 40 mg/mL oral suspension, authorised through the centralised procedure under product number EMEA/H/C/009412. The Agency is writing to confirm the terms and current status of a quality recommendation agreed during the initial marketing authorisation application. It also sets out what the Agency expects to receive and when.

The initial marketing authorisation application for Tirexafen oral suspension was submitted on 14 March 2025 under submission number EMEA/H/C/009412/0000. The Committee for Medicinal Products for Human Use (CHMP) assessed the quality module and raised a question about the in-use shelf life proposed for the multi-dose 150 mL presentation. The stability data provided at that time covered unopened containers under long-term and accelerated conditions. However, the data on the product after first opening were limited, and the proposed in-use period could not be fully supported. The applicant was invited to a clarification meeting held by teleconference on 2 September 2025 (meeting reference EMA/QWP/TC/2025/0457). At that meeting, the applicant agreed to generate confirmatory in-use data after authorisation. This was preferred to delaying the opinion. The commitment was then recorded formally in the applicant's responses submitted on 23 September 2025. The CHMP adopted a positive opinion on 16 October 2025, and the measure was included as a recommendation in the CHMP assessment report. The European Commission decision granting the marketing authorisation followed on 11 December 2025.

The marketing authorisation holder committed to complete an in-use stability study of the opened 150 mL Tirexafen oral suspension bottle. The study should mimic the conditions of patient use, including repeated opening and closing of the container and withdrawal of doses with the oral syringe supplied in the pack. It should also cover storage at the labelled temperature throughout the proposed in-use period. The Agency expects the report to cover at least two batches, one of which should be near the end of its approved shelf life at the time of opening. The report should present results for assay, degradation products, preservative content, pH, viscosity, resuspendability and microbiological quality, assessed against the finished product specification. The study report is due on 15 December 2026. This measure remains open and is not yet due. Marcus Ellery, Head of Stability Operations at Aldencourt, is named as the owner of this measure.

<!-- page 2 -->

Until the report has been assessed, the in-use period stated in section 6.3 of the Summary of Product Characteristics and in the package leaflet remains as currently authorised. If the study results support a change to the in-use period or to the storage instructions, the Agency expects the marketing authorisation holder to submit an appropriate variation application together with the PAM, or promptly after it. Updated product information should be included where needed.

Please submit the study report as a PAM through the eSubmission Gateway or Web Client, quoting procedure number EMEA/H/C/009412/REC/003 in the cover letter. If you expect the report to be delayed, please write to the Agency before the due date. Your letter should give a justification and a revised timeline so that the Agency can consider it in good time. The Agency should also be told without delay of any interim finding that could affect the quality of the product in the hands of patients. This includes out-of-specification results or signs of microbial growth. Any such finding should be notified through the established procedures and should not wait for the final report.

For questions about this measure, please contact the Product Lead named below at tirexafen.productlead@ema.europa.example or by telephone on +31 (0)88 781 6000, quoting reference EMA/PAM/2026/118734.

The Agency thanks Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals for its cooperation and looks forward to receiving the study report.

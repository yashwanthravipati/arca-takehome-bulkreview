# Registration Stability Protocol for Velorastat Film-Coated Tablets 25 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg

**Document number:** STAB-001
**Version:** 2.0
**Effective date:** 2023-05-10
**Status:** Current
**Product:** Product A, Velorastat film-coated tablets, 25 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg, in HDPE bottles with desiccant
**Document owner:** Regulatory Affairs CMC, Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This protocol defines the design, storage conditions, testing schedule, analytical methods and acceptance criteria for the registration stability study of Velorastat film-coated tablets 25 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg packaged in high-density polyethylene (HDPE) bottles with desiccant.

1.2 The data generated under this protocol are intended to support the proposed shelf life and storage statement in the marketing authorisation application and to provide the basis for the post-approval stability commitment. The study design follows ICH Q1A(R2), with reduced design elements justified in accordance with ICH Q1D and data evaluation performed in accordance with ICH Q1E.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This protocol applies to the registration (primary) stability batches of Velorastat film-coated tablets manufactured at the Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals Kestrel Park site, Building 4, and packaged in the commercial HDPE bottle presentation described in Section 4.

2.2 This protocol does not apply to:

- commercial batches placed on stability under the ongoing stability program, which are governed by STAB-002 (Ongoing Stability Program for Commercial Batches of Velorastat Film-Coated Tablets);
- batches packaged in Alu/Alu blisters, which are governed by STAB-003 (Stability Protocol Supporting Introduction of Alu/Alu Blister Packaging for Velorastat Film-Coated Tablets);
- drug substance stability, which is covered by separate documentation held by the drug substance manufacturer.

2.3 Release and shelf-life acceptance criteria for the drug product are defined in SPEC-A (Drug Product Specification: Velorastat Film-Coated Tablets 25 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg). Where this protocol and SPEC-A differ, the stricter requirement applies and the discrepancy shall be raised through change control.

## 4. Materials and batches

4.1 **Product description.** Velorastat film-coated tablets are immediate-release tablets. All three strengths are compressed from a common final blend at proportional tablet weights (100 mg, 200 mg and 400 mg core weight for the 25 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg strengths respectively) and coated with the same film-coating system at a common percentage weight gain.

4.2 **Container closure system.** 60 mL white HDPE bottle with a 33 mm polypropylene child-resistant closure, induction-sealed aluminium liner, and one 1 g silica gel desiccant canister. Each bottle contains 30 tablets. The container closure system is identical for all strengths.

4.3 **Registration batches.** Six registration batches are placed on study. Each batch is manufactured at pilot scale, at not less than one tenth of the proposed commercial batch size, using the commercial process and equipment train.

| Batch number | Strength | Common blend lot | Batch size (tablets) | Date of manufacture | Packaging date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| VLT25-R001 | 25 mg | VLB-2201 | 150,000 | 2022-06-14 | 2022-06-21 |
| VLT25-R002 | 25 mg | VLB-2202 | 150,000 | 2022-06-28 | 2022-07-05 |
| VLT25-R003 | 25 mg | VLB-2203 | 150,000 | 2022-07-12 | 2022-07-19 |
| VLT100-R001 | 100 mg | VLB-2201 | 40,000 | 2022-06-16 | 2022-06-23 |
| VLT100-R002 | 100 mg | VLB-2202 | 40,000 | 2022-06-30 | 2022-07-07 |
| VLT100-R003 | 100 mg | VLB-2203 | 40,000 | 2022-07-14 | 2022-07-21 |

4.4 Batches VLT25-R001 and VLT100-R001 are manufactured using drug substance lot VDS-22-014; the remaining batches use drug substance lots VDS-22-019 and VDS-22-023 respectively, so that at least two drug substance lots are represented across the study.

4.5 The study start date (time zero) for each batch is the date on which the samples are placed in the stability chambers, which shall be no more than 30 days after packaging.

## 5. Analytical methods

5.1 All testing shall be performed using validated, stability-indicating methods. Validation was performed in accordance with ICH Q2(R1), and forced degradation studies have demonstrated that VLR-ketone, the principal oxidative degradation product, is resolved from velorastat and from all other known impurities.

| Test | Method number | Technique |
|---|---|---|
| Appearance | AM-VLR-010 | Visual inspection |
| Identification | AM-VLR-004 | HPLC retention time and UV spectrum |
| Assay | AM-VLR-001 | Reversed-phase HPLC with UV detection at 275 nm |
| Related substances (VLR-ketone, VLR-desmethyl, unspecified impurities, total impurities) | AM-VLR-001 | Reversed-phase HPLC with UV detection at 275 nm |
| Dissolution | AM-VLR-002 | USP Apparatus 2 (paddle), 75 rpm, 900 mL 0.05 M phosphate buffer pH 6.8; HPLC finish |
| Water content | AM-VLR-003 | Coulometric Karl Fischer |
| Hardness | AM-VLR-011 | Tablet hardness tester (information only) |
| Microbial enumeration | SOP-QC-0412 | Ph. Eur. 2.6.12 / USP <61> |
| Specified microorganisms (*E. coli*) | SOP-QC-0413 | Ph. Eur. 2.6.13 / USP <62> |

5.2 Identification is performed at time zero only. Microbial testing is performed at time zero and at 12, 24 and 36 months at the long-term condition.

5.3 Where an analytical method is revised during the study, a method comparability assessment shall be completed under change control before the revised method is applied to stability samples.

## 6. Storage conditions

6.1 Registration batches will be stored at the long-term condition of 25°C ± 2°C / 60% RH ± 5% RH and at the accelerated condition of 40°C ± 2°C / 75% RH ± 5% RH. Samples are stored upright in the final commercial container closure system.

| Condition | Chamber ID | Location | Monitoring |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long-term, 25°C ± 2°C / 60% RH ± 5% RH | SC-07 | QC Building 2, Room 2.14 | Continuous, Vaisala viewLinc, alarm per SOP-ENG-0331 |
| Accelerated, 40°C ± 2°C / 75% RH ± 5% RH | SC-09 | QC Building 2, Room 2.14 | Continuous, Vaisala viewLinc, alarm per SOP-ENG-0331 |
| Intermediate (contingency), 30°C ± 2°C / 65% RH ± 5% RH | SC-11 | QC Building 2, Room 2.16 | Continuous, Vaisala viewLinc, alarm per SOP-ENG-0331 |

6.2 **Intermediate condition.** Sufficient samples are placed at the intermediate condition at study start. These samples are tested only if a significant change, as defined in ICH Q1A(R2), occurs at the accelerated condition. In that case, the intermediate samples are tested at 0, 6, 9 and 12 months, and the outcome is assessed by Pharmaceutical Development and Regulatory Affairs CMC.

6.3 **Photostability.** Confirmatory photostability testing is performed once, on batch VLT100-R001 as unpackaged tablets and in the immediate pack, according to ICH Q1B Option 2, with an overall illumination of not less than 1.2 million lux hours and an integrated near-ultraviolet energy of not less than 200 watt hours per square metre. Photostability samples are tested for appearance, assay, related substances and dissolution.

6.4 Chambers are qualified per SOP-ENG-0320 and mapped annually. Excursions are handled according to Section 9.

## 7. Testing schedule

7.1 Long-term samples will be tested at 0, 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, 24 and 36 months, and accelerated samples at 0, 3 and 6 months. Time zero results are shared between conditions and are generated from samples taken at the time of chamber placement.

7.2 **Pull window.** Samples shall be removed from the chamber within ±7 days of the nominal pull date for timepoints up to and including 6 months, and within ±14 days for later timepoints. Testing shall start within 14 days of removal from the chamber; samples awaiting testing are stored at 2–8°C in their unopened containers.

## 8. Acceptance criteria

8.1 Stability acceptance criteria are consistent with the shelf-life criteria in SPEC-A. At every stability timepoint the tablets must meet assay 95.0–105.0% of label claim, VLR-ketone not more than 0.3%, total impurities not more than 1.0%, and dissolution not less than 80% (Q) in 30 minutes.

| Test | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|
| Appearance | 25 mg: white, round, biconvex film-coated tablet debossed "V25"; 50 mg: yellow, oval, debossed "V50"; 100 mg: pink, oval, debossed "V100" |
| Assay | 95.0–105.0% of label claim |
| VLR-ketone | NMT 0.3% |
| VLR-desmethyl | NMT 0.2% |
| Any unspecified impurity | NMT 0.10% |
| Total impurities | NMT 1.0% |
| Dissolution | NLT 80% (Q) in 30 minutes |
| Water content | NMT 4.0% w/w |
| Hardness | Report result |
| Total aerobic microbial count | NMT 10³ CFU/g |
| Total combined yeasts and moulds count | NMT 10² CFU/g |
| *Escherichia coli* | Absent in 1 g |

8.2 Impurity thresholds for reporting, identification and qualification follow ICH Q3B(R2). Impurities are reported to two decimal places for results below 1.0% and to one decimal place for results of 1.0% and above.

<!-- page 5 -->

8.3 **Out-of-trend evaluation.** Assay and VLR-ketone results are trended in LIMS against the batch's regression line. A result outside the 95% prediction interval, or a VLR-ketone result increasing by more than 0.10% between consecutive timepoints, is treated as out-of-trend and investigated per SOP-QC-0224.

8.4 **Shelf-life evaluation.** Shelf life is evaluated in accordance with ICH Q1E using regression analysis of assay and VLR-ketone data at the long-term condition, with batch poolability tested at the 0.25 significance level.

## 9. Deviation handling

9.1 Any departure from this protocol, including missed or late pulls, chamber excursions, incorrect sample placement, or the use of a method version other than that specified, shall be recorded as a deviation in the electronic quality management system according to SOP-QA-0105, Deviation Management.

9.2 **Chamber excursions.** Excursions outside the specified temperature or humidity ranges are assessed by Facilities and Engineering and Quality Assurance per SOP-ENG-0331. Excursions of 24 hours or less that remain within ±5°C and ±10% RH of the set point are documented and assessed for impact; excursions exceeding these limits require a formal deviation and an impact assessment by Pharmaceutical Development. If the impact cannot be justified, affected samples may be transferred to a backup chamber (SC-08 for long-term, SC-10 for accelerated) and the data annotated.

9.3 **Out-of-specification results.** OOS results are investigated per SOP-QC-0220. Confirmed OOS results at any timepoint are reported to Regulatory Affairs CMC within two working days for assessment of regulatory reporting obligations.

9.4 **Protocol amendments.** Planned changes to the study design require a protocol revision approved by the signatories in Section 14 before implementation.

9.5 **Deviation record.** No deviations have been recorded against this protocol as of the effective date of this version. Any future deviations will be listed in the final stability report together with their impact assessments.

## 10. Bracketing and matrixing

10.1 Bracketing is applied so that only the 25 mg and 100 mg strengths are placed on study, because all three strengths are compressed from a common blend at proportional tablet weights and are packaged in the same HDPE bottle presentation. The 50 mg strength is therefore represented by the extremes, in accordance with ICH Q1D.

10.2 The bracketing design is supported by the following:

- identical qualitative composition and proportional quantitative composition of the tablet cores;
- a common film-coating system applied at the same percentage weight gain;
- identical manufacturing process, equipment train and in-process controls;
- identical container closure system (60 mL HDPE bottle, 30 tablets, 1 g silica gel desiccant), so that the headspace-to-tablet ratio is bounded by the 25 mg and 100 mg strengths;
- development stability data at 40°C / 75% RH showing comparable degradation rates for all three strengths.

<!-- page 6 -->

10.3 If the data from either extreme strength show behaviour that differs materially from the other, or if any extreme fails to meet the acceptance criteria, the 50 mg strength shall be assumed to behave no better than the worse extreme until data from the 50 mg strength are generated.

10.4 Matrixing is not applied. All scheduled tests are performed on all batches at all timepoints.

## 11. Post-approval commitments

11.1 The applicant commits to placing the first three production-scale batches of the 25 mg and 100 mg strengths on long-term stability through 36 months and reporting the results in the annual report.

11.2 The commitment batches will be tested according to the long-term schedule, methods and acceptance criteria of this protocol. Regulatory Affairs CMC is responsible for tracking the commitment and for including the data in each annual report until it is fulfilled.

11.3 After the commitment is fulfilled, commercial batches are placed on stability under STAB-002. Any change to the container closure system, including the introduction of Alu/Alu blisters, is supported separately under STAB-003 and does not affect this commitment.

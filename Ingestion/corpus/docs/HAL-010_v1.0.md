# Closure of Post-Authorisation Measure: Extractables and Leachables Study for Tirexafen Oral Suspension (EMEA/H/C/009412)

European Medicines Agency, Domenico Scarlattilaan 6, 1083 HS Amsterdam, The Netherlands

Date: 27 February 2026

Our reference: EMA/PAM/71842/2026

Procedure number: EMEA/H/C/009412/REC/003

Product: Tirexafen 40 mg/mL oral suspension, 150 mL amber glass bottle

Dr. Lena Vogt

Regulatory Affairs Manager

Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals

Kaiserstrasse 118, 60329 Frankfurt am Main, Germany

Dear Dr. Vogt,

I am writing about the post-authorisation measure for Tirexafen 40 mg/mL oral suspension. This medicinal product was authorised in the European Union under the centralised procedure with procedure number EMEA/H/C/009412. The measure is recorded under procedure number EMEA/H/C/009412/REC/003. This letter confirms the outcome of the Agency's assessment and closes the measure.

During the initial marketing authorisation procedure, the Committee for Medicinal Products for Human Use (CHMP) reviewed the quality data for the container closure system, a 150 mL Type III amber glass bottle with a child-resistant polypropylene closure. The extractables and leachables data available at the time of the opinion covered 12 months of real-time storage. That was enough to support authorisation but did not cover the full proposed shelf life of 24 months. At the CHMP plenary meeting held on 16 to 19 June 2025, the Committee adopted a positive opinion together with a recommendation for a post-authorisation measure. The measure required the full-duration leachables data to be provided once the study was complete. The Commission Decision granting the marketing authorisation followed on 21 August 2025. The measure was entered into the post-authorisation measures register with the agreed timeline.

The marketing authorisation holder committed to submit the final report of the extractables and leachables study for the Tirexafen amber glass bottle. The final report was due on 31 January 2026. Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals submitted the report through the eSubmission Gateway on 22 January 2026, under submission sequence 0007. This was before the agreed deadline. The submission included the complete extractables profile of the glass, the closure and the liner, obtained under exaggerated extraction conditions. It also included the leachables results for three primary batches stored for 24 months at 25 °C/60% RH and for 6 months at 40 °C/75% RH.

The rapporteur's quality assessor reviewed the report. The assessor noted that all identified leachables were below the analytical evaluation threshold, or were within the toxicologically qualified limits based on the maximum daily dose. Elemental leachables from the glass, including aluminium, boron and arsenic, stayed within the permitted daily exposures set out in ICH Q3D. No new or unexpected compounds were observed at the later time points. The data support the approved shelf life of 24 months and the currently registered storage conditions. The Agency considers this measure fulfilled. No changes to the product information or to the quality sections of the dossier are needed as a result of this assessment. The measure will be shown as closed in the post-authorisation measures register.

Dr. Lena Vogt, Regulatory Affairs Manager at Aldencourt, is named as the owner of this measure. Please keep the study records and raw data available for inspection by the competent authorities. Any future change to the container closure system, such as a change in glass supplier, closure material or liner composition, must be submitted through the appropriate variation procedure. Such an application should include an assessment of its effect on the extractables and leachables profile. No further action is required from Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals for this measure.

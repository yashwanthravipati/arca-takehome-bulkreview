# Registration Stability Protocol for Tirexafen Oral Suspension 40 mg/mL

Document number: STAB-004
Version: 2.1
Effective date: 2023-09-14
Status: Current
Product: Product B, Tirexafen oral suspension 40 mg/mL in 150 mL amber glass bottles
Document type: Stability protocol
Owner: Stability Sciences, Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals, Hollins Bay Site

## 1. Purpose

This protocol defines the design, conduct and evaluation of the registration stability study for Tirexafen oral suspension 40 mg/mL. The study generates the primary stability data supporting the marketing authorisation application. These data will be used to propose a shelf life and storage statement for the commercial product in its intended container closure system.

The study is designed in accordance with ICH Q1A(R2). Data will be evaluated in accordance with ICH Q1E. Photostability testing is included as described in Section 7.4 and follows ICH Q1B.

## 2. Scope

This protocol applies to the three primary registration batches of Tirexafen oral suspension 40 mg/mL manufactured at the Aldencourt Hollins Bay Site and packaged in the proposed commercial container closure system. It covers the following activities:

- placement of samples in storage
- sample pulls and testing at the specified time points
- evaluation of results against the acceptance criteria
- handling of deviations and out-of-specification results
- reporting of data for regulatory submission

The following studies are outside the scope of this protocol and are governed by separate controlled documents:

- Stability of batches manufactured at the Riverton Site is covered by STAB-005.
- In-use stability of the product after first opening of the bottle is covered by STAB-006.

The release and shelf-life specification for the drug product is defined in SPEC-B. Where the two documents differ, the shelf-life acceptance criteria in Section 8 of this protocol apply to stability samples.

## 4. Materials and batches

### 4.1 Product description

Tirexafen oral suspension 40 mg/mL is a white to off-white, strawberry-flavoured, homogeneous suspension after shaking. Each mL contains 40 mg of tirexafen. Sodium benzoate is included as the antimicrobial preservative at a labelled amount of 2.0 mg/mL. The suspending system is microcrystalline cellulose and carmellose sodium with xanthan gum.

### 4.2 Container closure system

The product is filled at a nominal 150 mL into 150 mL Type III amber glass bottles (component code PK-0571). Each bottle is closed with a 28 mm child-resistant polypropylene cap with an induction-sealed aluminium foil liner (component code PK-0584). The container closure system used in the study is identical to the proposed commercial presentation. A 5 mL oral dosing syringe is co-packaged in the commercial carton but is not placed on stability.

## 5. Analytical methods

All methods are validated per ICH Q2(R1) and shown to be stability-indicating where applicable. The current effective version of each method at the time of testing is used.

| Test | Method number | Technique |
|---|---|---|
| Appearance | AM-TXF-001 | Visual inspection |
| Redispersibility | AM-TXF-003 | Inversion count to homogeneity |
| Assay of tirexafen | AM-TXF-012 | Reversed-phase HPLC-UV, 254 nm |
| Related substances (TXF-acid, unspecified, total) | AM-TXF-013 | Reversed-phase HPLC-UV, gradient |
| Sodium benzoate content | AM-TXF-015 | Reversed-phase HPLC-UV, 230 nm |
| pH | AM-GEN-004 | Potentiometry at 25°C |
| Viscosity | AM-TXF-021 | Rotational viscometer, spindle LV-2, 30 rpm |
| Particle size distribution | AM-TXF-024 | Laser diffraction |
| Deliverable volume | AM-GEN-011 | Gravimetric |
| Microbial enumeration | AM-MIC-002 | Ph. Eur. 2.6.12 / USP <61> |
| Specified microorganisms | AM-MIC-003 | Ph. Eur. 2.6.13 / USP <62> |
| Antimicrobial preservative efficacy | AM-MIC-008 | Ph. Eur. 5.1.3 / USP <51> |

Before sampling for assay, related substances and sodium benzoate, each bottle is redispersed per AM-TXF-003. Results are reported to the number of decimal places stated in the acceptance criteria.

## 6. Storage conditions

Stability chambers are qualified per SOP-ENG-0215. Each chamber is continuously monitored by the validated Rees Centron environmental monitoring system. Temperature and relative humidity are logged at 5-minute intervals. Alarms trigger when readings deviate by more than ±2°C or ±5% RH for longer than 15 minutes.

Tirexafen oral suspension in 150 mL amber glass bottles will be stored upright at 25°C ± 2°C / 60% RH ± 5% RH (long-term) and at 40°C ± 2°C / 75% RH ± 5% RH (accelerated). Long-term samples are held in chamber CH-04. Accelerated samples are held in chamber CH-07. Both chambers are located in Building 3, Room 3.114, at the Hollins Bay Site.

Upright orientation was selected for the following reasons:

- Upright is the orientation in which the product will be stored by patients and pharmacies.
- The induction-sealed liner prevents contact between the suspension and the polypropylene cap material in any orientation.

An intermediate condition of 30°C ± 2°C / 65% RH ± 5% RH in chamber CH-05 is reserved as a contingency. Samples are placed at the intermediate condition at study start. They are tested only if a significant change, as defined in ICH Q1A(R2), occurs at any time point under accelerated storage. If triggered, intermediate testing is performed at 0, 6, 9 and 12 months. Untested intermediate samples are discarded at study close-out.

## 7. Testing schedule

### 7.1 Time points

Testing will be performed at 0, 3, 6, 9, 12, 18 and 24 months under long-term storage and at 0, 3 and 6 months under accelerated storage.

The pull window is ±7 days for time points up to and including 6 months, and ±14 days for later time points. Pulled samples are tested within 30 days of the pull date. Testing at the accelerated 6-month time point and at the long-term 12-month time point must be completed before the interim report is issued.

At the 3-month and 6-month accelerated time points, the following tests are performed:

- appearance
- redispersibility
- assay
- related substances
- sodium benzoate
- pH

Particle size distribution is additionally tested at 6 months. Microbiological testing is not performed under accelerated storage.

Batch TXS-23-011 is tested per ICH Q1B Option 2 in photostability cabinet PS-02. Three sample sets are exposed:

- product removed from the bottle and exposed in a quartz dish
- product in the immediate pack (amber bottle)
- dark controls wrapped in aluminium foil

## 8. Acceptance criteria

Each sample must comply with assay 95.0–105.0% of label claim, TXF-acid not more than 0.5%, total impurities not more than 1.5%, pH between 4.5 and 5.5, and sodium benzoate 80.0–110.0% of the labelled amount. These shelf-life criteria apply to all stability time points and conditions. Release limits in SPEC-B are tighter for some attributes and do not apply to stability samples. For example, the release limit for sodium benzoate is 90.0–110.0% of the labelled amount.

| Test | Shelf-life acceptance criterion |
|---|---|
| Appearance | White to off-white suspension, free from caking after shaking |
| Redispersibility | Homogeneous within 10 inversions |
| Assay of tirexafen | 95.0–105.0% of label claim |
| TXF-acid | NMT 0.5% |
| TXF-dimer | NMT 0.3% |
| Any unspecified impurity | NMT 0.20% |
| Total impurities | NMT 1.5% |
| pH | 4.5–5.5 |
| Sodium benzoate | 80.0–110.0% of labelled amount |
| Viscosity | 400–900 mPa·s |
| Particle size | D90 NMT 45 µm |
| Deliverable volume | Mean NLT 150 mL; no individual bottle less than 145 mL |
| TAMC | NMT 10² CFU/mL |
| TYMC | NMT 10¹ CFU/mL |
| Escherichia coli | Absent in 1 mL |
| Preservative efficacy | Meets Ph. Eur. 5.1.3 criteria for oral preparations |

Impurity results are expressed as percentage area relative to tirexafen. Reporting, identification and qualification thresholds are 0.10%, 0.20% and 0.20%, respectively.

Shelf-life estimation for assay, TXF-acid, total impurities and sodium benzoate is performed by regression analysis. The analysis uses the one-sided 95% confidence limit and tests batch poolability at a significance level of 0.25, as described in ICH Q1E. Extrapolation beyond the available long-term data is limited to that permitted by ICH Q1E.

## 9. Deviation handling

### 9.1 General

Any departure from this protocol is recorded as a deviation in the QMS (TrackWise) per SOP-QA-0107. Typical departures include missed pull windows, chamber excursions, sample mix-ups and testing outside the defined window.

Chamber excursions are handled as follows:

- Excursions within ±2°C or ±5% RH of the setpoint lasting no more than 24 hours are documented but do not require an impact assessment.
- Excursions beyond these limits require a documented impact assessment by Quality Assurance.

OOS results are investigated per SOP-QC-0142. OOT results are identified using the regression-based prediction intervals defined in SOP-STB-0031. All OOS and OOT investigations are referenced in the stability report.

### 9.2 Deviations recorded during the study

Deviation DEV-2023-0311 documented a humidity excursion to 81% RH lasting 14 hours in accelerated chamber CH-07, and the affected 3-month samples were judged unaffected based on the container closure system. The excursion occurred between 2023-06-02 and 2023-06-03. Its root cause was a failed humidifier solenoid valve, which was replaced under work order WO-ENG-23-0947.

The assessment concluded that the induction-sealed, tightly capped amber glass bottle gives negligible moisture ingress over a 14-hour period. The 3-month accelerated results for all three batches were within acceptance criteria and consistent with the trend observed at T=0. No temperature excursion occurred during the event. QA closed DEV-2023-0311 on 2023-07-19 with no impact on study validity, and the 3-month samples were not replaced.

## 10. Bracketing and matrixing

Bracketing is not applied to this study because Tirexafen oral suspension is registered in a single strength and a single 150 mL container size. Matrixing is also not applied. All batches are tested at all time points for the tests listed in Section 7, so a full design is used.

Any future introduction of an additional fill volume or strength will require a protocol amendment. That amendment will include a design assessment per ICH Q1D.

## 11. Post-approval commitments

Stability data available at the time of submission will cover at least 12 months of long-term storage and 6 months of accelerated storage on all three registration batches. The proposed shelf life is 24 months, with the storage statement "Do not store above 25°C. Do not freeze."

The applicant commits to continuing the three registration batches through 24 months and to placing the first three commercial batches on long-term stability through the proposed 24-month shelf life. Commercial batches will be tested using the long-term schedule and acceptance criteria in this protocol, or in an equivalent approved protocol.

Following completion of the commitment batches, one commercial batch per year will be placed on long-term stability as part of the ongoing stability programme per SOP-STB-0040. Any confirmed OOS result in a commitment or annual batch will be reported to the competent authorities as required by the applicable regulations.

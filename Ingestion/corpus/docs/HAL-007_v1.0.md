# Post-Authorisation Measure for Velorastat Film-Coated Tablets (EMEA/H/C/009288): Elemental Impurities Risk Assessment

European Medicines Agency, Domenico Scarlattilaan 6, 1083 HS Amsterdam, The Netherlands

Amsterdam, 14 November 2025

Our reference: EMA/PAM/752014/2025
Procedure number: EMEA/H/C/009288/MEA/002
Product: Velorastat film-coated tablets, 25 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg

Dr. Priya Raman, Associate Director of Regulatory Affairs, Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals, Regulatory Affairs Department, 41 Harrowgate Business Park, Dublin 24, Ireland

Dear Dr. Raman,

We are writing about a post-authorisation measure for Velorastat film-coated tablets, centrally authorised under procedure EMEA/H/C/009288. This letter confirms the measure's scope, due date and current status, and sets out what the Agency expects from your company before the deadline.

The marketing authorisation application for Velorastat 25 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg film-coated tablets, packaged in high-density polyethylene bottles with desiccant, was validated on 23 October 2024. During the assessment, the Rapporteur's quality evaluation found that the dossier did not include a complete risk assessment of elemental impurities in the finished product. The dossier contained batch data for a limited set of elements in the active substance. However, it did not systematically evaluate potential contributions from the excipients, the manufacturing equipment, the water used in processing or the container closure system, including the desiccant canister. The Committee for Medicinal Products for Human Use (CHMP) raised this point as an "other concern" in the Day 120 List of Questions.

In your company's response, submitted on 3 April 2025, Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals proposed to address this point after authorisation. The CHMP discussed the matter at its June 2025 plenary meeting and accepted the proposal. It concluded that the available data supported a positive benefit-risk balance and that the outstanding information could be provided as a post-authorisation measure. The measure was recorded in the CHMP assessment report adopted on 26 June 2025. The European Commission granted the marketing authorisation by decision on 28 August 2025.

The marketing authorisation holder committed to submit an elemental impurities risk assessment for Velorastat film-coated tablets in line with ICH Q3D. The assessment should cover all three strengths and the authorised packaging configuration. It should identify and evaluate all potential sources of elemental impurities. Where the assessment does not show that levels are consistently below 30% of the permitted daily exposure, it should include supporting analytical data from representative commercial-scale batches. The assessment should also state the control strategy that will apply. If changes to the finished product specification are needed, these should be submitted through the appropriate variation procedure and referenced in your submission.

<!-- page 2 -->

The risk assessment is due on 28 February 2026. As of the date of this letter, the Agency has not received the submission. This measure remains open. We note that the due date has not yet passed. This letter is therefore a confirmation of the commitment and a reminder, not a notice of non-fulfilment. Dr. Priya Raman, Associate Director of Regulatory Affairs at Aldencourt, is named as the owner of this measure. Please let us know promptly if responsibility for this measure changes within your organisation.

Please submit the risk assessment through the eSubmission Gateway as a post-authorisation measure under procedure number EMEA/H/C/009288/MEA/002, using the electronic Common Technical Document format. The assessment should be placed in Module 3, with a cover letter that cross-references this correspondence. After receipt, the Rapporteur will assess the submission. The outcome will be communicated to you once the CHMP has reviewed it. If the assessment raises further questions, a request for supplementary information may be issued.

If your company expects any difficulty in meeting the due date, please contact us well before 28 February 2026. Your request should give a justification and a proposed revised timeline, which the CHMP will consider. Questions about this letter may be addressed to the Product Lead, Mr. Tomasz Wieczorek, at velorastat.pl@ema.example.eu, quoting reference EMA/PAM/752014/2025.

Thank you for your continued cooperation.

Yours sincerely,

Dr. Annelies Verbruggen

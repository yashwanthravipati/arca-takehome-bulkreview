# Drug Product Specification: Velorastat Film-Coated Tablets 25 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg

**Document number:** SPEC-A  
**Version:** 4.0  
**Effective date:** 2025-02-03  
**Status:** Current  
**Document type:** Specification  
**Owner:** Quality Control, Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals, Northfield Site

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This specification defines the tests, analytical procedures and acceptance criteria for Velorastat Film-Coated Tablets 25 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg (Product A). It applies to batch release and to testing throughout the assigned shelf life.

1.2 The specification ensures that each batch released to market, and each batch tested on stability, is of consistent quality and is fit for its intended use. Product quality must be consistent with the registered dossier and with the batches used in clinical and registration stability studies.

1.3 This document is the controlling reference for Quality Control (QC) testing and for Quality Assurance (QA) disposition decisions. It is also the reference for out-of-specification (OOS) investigations conducted under SOP-QC-0142, Investigation of Out-of-Specification and Out-of-Trend Results.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This specification applies to all commercial batches of Velorastat Film-Coated Tablets 25 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg. It covers batches manufactured at the Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals Northfield Site (Building 4, Oral Solid Dose Suite) and packaged in high-density polyethylene (HDPE) bottles with desiccant.

2.2 The specification applies to:

- release testing of finished, packaged product;
- stability testing performed under STAB-001, Registration Stability Protocol for Velorastat Film-Coated Tablets 25 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg;
- stability testing performed under STAB-002, Ongoing Stability Program for Commercial Batches of Velorastat Film-Coated Tablets;
- retest of retained samples where required by an investigation or complaint.

2.3 The Alu/Alu blister presentation under evaluation in STAB-003, Stability Protocol Supporting Introduction of Alu/Alu Blister Packaging for Velorastat Film-Coated Tablets, is outside the scope of this version. Any extension of this specification to the blister presentation shall be managed through change control under SOP-QA-0031, Change Control, once STAB-003 data are available and reviewed.

2.4 This specification does not cover the velorastat drug substance, excipients, bulk (unpackaged) tablets or in-process controls. These are controlled through separate drug substance specifications, raw material specifications and batch manufacturing records.

## 3. Product description and composition

3.1 **Description.** Velorastat Film-Coated Tablets are immediate-release, film-coated tablets for oral administration. The three strengths are dose-proportional with respect to the tablet core and are distinguished by size, colour and debossing:

| Strength | Shape and size | Colour | Debossing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 mg | Round, biconvex, 6.0 mm | Yellow | "AV" on one side, "25" on the other |
| 50 mg | Round, biconvex, 8.0 mm | Pink | "AV" on one side, "50" on the other |
| 100 mg | Oval, biconvex, 14.0 × 7.0 mm | White to off-white | "AV" on one side, "100" on the other |

3.3 **Container closure system.** The tablets are packed in white 60 mL HDPE bottles containing 30 tablets. Each bottle includes a 1 g silica gel desiccant canister and is closed with a child-resistant polypropylene cap with an induction-sealed aluminium liner. A 90-tablet presentation in a 150 mL HDPE bottle with a 2 g silica gel canister is also registered.

3.4 **Storage and shelf life.** Store below 30 °C. Store in the original package in order to protect from moisture. The approved shelf life is 36 months. This is supported by long-term data at 30 °C/75% RH and 25 °C/60% RH, and by accelerated data at 40 °C/75% RH, generated under STAB-001.

Microbiological quality is tested annually on stability under STAB-002 at the 12-, 24- and 36-month time points.

## 5. Justification of specification

5.1 **General.** The acceptance criteria were set in accordance with ICH Q6A. They take account of the clinical and toxicological qualification of impurities, the capability of the manufacturing process, and data from 14 registration and commercial batches across the three strengths, comprising 4 batches of 25 mg, 5 batches of 50 mg and 5 batches of 100 mg. Stability data from STAB-001 at 36 months and from STAB-002 commercial batches up to 24 months were also considered.

5.2 **Assay.** The 95.0–105.0% range applies at both release and end of shelf life. Release results across all batches ranged from 98.6% to 101.4% of label claim. The mean decrease at 36 months at 30 °C/75% RH was 1.2% of label claim. The upper and lower limits therefore provide adequate margin over the observed process and analytical variability, which has a pooled intermediate precision of 0.6% RSD.

5.3 **VLR-ketone.** VLR-ketone is the only specified degradation product. It forms by oxidation of the secondary alcohol of velorastat and increases slowly at elevated humidity.

- *Batch and stability data.* At release, levels ranged from 0.04% to 0.09%. The highest value observed at 36 months, 30 °C/75% RH, was 0.19%.
- *Release limit.* The release limit of NMT 0.2% allows for the maximum observed increase on storage while assuring compliance at expiry.
- *Shelf-life limit and qualification.* The maximum daily dose is 200 mg, so the ICH Q3B(R2) qualification threshold is 0.2%. The shelf-life limit of NMT 0.3% therefore exceeds that threshold and requires separate qualification. VLR-ketone is qualified by the 26-week rat and 39-week dog toxicity studies (reports TOX-VLR-2207 and TOX-VLR-2211), in which the impurity was present at levels corresponding to 0.8% of the dose. It is also a major circulating human metabolite.

<!-- page 5 -->

5.4 **Unspecified impurities.** Under ICH Q3B(R2), the identification threshold for a 200 mg maximum daily dose is 0.2%. The limit of NMT 0.10% is deliberately tighter than this threshold.

- *Data supporting the limit.* No unspecified degradation product has exceeded 0.06% in any batch or at any stability time point under STAB-001 or STAB-002.
- *History.* In version 4.0 the shelf-life limit was tightened from NMT 0.20% to NMT 0.10% to reflect accumulated data and to improve sensitivity to process or packaging changes.
- *Process impurities.* Velorastat process impurities, including VLR-desmethyl and VLR-chloro intermediate, are controlled in the drug substance specification. They are not degradation products and are not reported in the drug product test.

5.5 **Total impurities.** Total impurities at release did not exceed 0.21%, and the highest result at 36 months was 0.38%. The release limit of NMT 0.7% and the shelf-life limit of NMT 1.0% accommodate the specified degradant at its limit together with several unspecified impurities near the reporting threshold.

5.6 **Dissolution.** Velorastat is a BCS Class II substance whose solubility depends on pH.

- *Medium.* The medium was selected because it provides sink conditions while remaining discriminating.
- *Discriminating power.* Batches manufactured with granule moisture outside the proven acceptable range, and with over-lubrication (8-minute versus 3-minute blending), were clearly distinguished.
- *Batch data.* All clinical and registration batches released NLT 91% at 30 minutes, with no downward trend on storage.
- *Limit.* The limit of Q = 80% at 30 minutes is consistent with the pivotal bioequivalence batch profile.

5.7 **Water content.** Water content correlates with VLR-ketone formation. Release water content ranged from 2.1% to 2.9% w/w and increased by no more than 0.9% w/w at 36 months in the desiccated HDPE bottle. The release limit of NMT 3.5% w/w and shelf-life limit of NMT 4.5% w/w therefore act as a supporting control on chemical stability.

5.8 **Other tests.**

- *Uniformity of dosage units.* Content uniformity is applied to all strengths in accordance with pharmacopoeial requirements.
- *Residual solvents.* Ethanol is the only solvent used in drug substance manufacture that carries over, with batch results NMT 410 ppm. It is controlled at release against the ICH Q3C Option 1 limit.
- *Microbiological quality.* Microbiological limits follow Ph. Eur. 5.1.4 for non-aqueous oral preparations. The reduced frequency is justified by low water activity (aw ≤ 0.45) and a history of results below 10 CFU/g.

## 6. Analytical procedures

6.1 All analytical procedures listed in Section 4 are validated in accordance with ICH Q2(R2), or are verified as compendial methods under SOP-QC-0155, Verification of Compendial Procedures. Validation reports are held in the QC document management system under the method number.

<!-- page 6 -->

6.2 **AM-VLR-001, Assay, identification and degradation products by HPLC.**

- *Column and temperature.* Reversed-phase gradient HPLC on a C18 column (150 × 4.6 mm, 3.5 µm) at 35 °C.
- *Mobile phase.* Mobile phase A is 0.1% phosphoric acid in water; mobile phase B is acetonitrile.
- *Flow and injection.* Flow rate 1.0 mL/min; injection volume 10 µL.
- *Detection.* UV at 245 nm, with diode-array detection from 210 to 400 nm for identification.
- *Sample and standard.* The sample solution is a composite of 10 tablets at a nominal concentration of 0.5 mg/mL velorastat. Assay is calculated against the velorastat reference standard. VLR-ketone is quantified using a relative response factor of 1.12. Unspecified impurities are quantified using a relative response factor of 1.00.
- *Sensitivity.* The limit of quantitation is 0.03%.
- *System suitability.* Resolution between velorastat and VLR-ketone is NLT 2.0. Repeatability of six standard injections is NMT 1.0% RSD.

6.3 **AM-VLR-002, Dissolution.**

- *Apparatus and conditions.* USP Apparatus 2 (paddle) at 75 rpm, with 900 mL of 0.05 M sodium phosphate buffer pH 6.8 containing 0.5% w/v sodium lauryl sulfate, at 37.0 ± 0.5 °C.
- *Sampling and analysis.* Samples are withdrawn at 30 minutes, filtered through a 10 µm full-flow filter, and analysed by UV spectrophotometry at 245 nm against a standard of known concentration.
- *Acceptance.* Stage acceptance follows Ph. Eur. 2.9.3 / USP <711>.

6.4 **AM-VLR-003, Uniformity of dosage units.** Individual tablets are dissolved in diluent and analysed using the HPLC conditions of AM-VLR-001 under an isocratic assay-only run. The acceptance value is calculated per Ph. Eur. 2.9.40 / USP <905>.

6.5 **AM-VLR-004, Water content.** Volumetric Karl Fischer titration of crushed tablet composite, sample size 200 mg, using methanol/formamide (2:1) as the working medium.

6.6 **AM-VLR-005, Residual solvents.** Static headspace gas chromatography with flame ionisation detection, per Ph. Eur. 2.4.24 / USP <467>, validated for ethanol.

6.7 **AM-VLR-010, Appearance.** Visual examination of 20 tablets under white light against a white background, with confirmation of debossing using a 10× magnifier.

6.8 **AM-MIC-001, Microbiological examination of non-sterile products.** Plate count and specified-organism tests per Ph. Eur. 2.6.12 and 2.6.13 / USP <61> and <62>. Method suitability is demonstrated with 1:10 dilution in buffered sodium chloride–peptone solution containing 1% polysorbate 80.

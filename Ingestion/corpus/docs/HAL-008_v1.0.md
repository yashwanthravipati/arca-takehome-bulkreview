# Condition of Authorisation: Calvidronate Concentrate for Infusion (Authorisation 69802)

Swissmedic, Swiss Agency for Therapeutic Products, Authorisation Division, Hallerstrasse 7, 3012 Bern, Switzerland

Bern, 3 March 2026

Our reference: SMC-AUF-2026-0412. Authorisation number: 69802. Related case: SMC-ZL-2024-18837.

Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals, Attention: Mr Marcus Ellery, Head of Stability Operations, Regulatory Correspondence Office

Dear Mr Ellery,

We are writing about a condition attached to the marketing authorisation for Calvidronate concentrate for solution for infusion 5 mg/mL (authorisation number 69802). The product is supplied in 4 mL, 10 mL and 20 mL Type I glass vials and is stored refrigerated. This letter sets out the condition, its due date and its current status, and explains how we expect the outstanding information to be provided.

Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals submitted the application for Calvidronate on 14 October 2024. During the scientific assessment, our quality assessors reviewed the container closure integrity data in the quality dossier. These data covered the 20 mL vial presentation tested with a deterministic method. For the 4 mL and 10 mL presentations, the dossier relied on bracketing arguments and on sterility results from the stability programme. Our assessors considered that this approach was not sufficient on its own. The smaller vials use a different stopper size and crimp configuration, and the product is held at 2 °C to 8 °C for its whole shelf life. Under those conditions, stopper compression set and seal performance over time cannot be inferred reliably from the largest presentation.

We raised this point in our List of Questions dated 20 March 2025. It was then discussed at a clarification meeting with your quality and regulatory representatives on 22 May 2025 (meeting reference SMC-CM-2025-0961). At that meeting, Aldencourt explained that a validated deterministic leak test method for the smaller vials was being transferred to its contract testing laboratory. Aldencourt proposed to provide the results after approval rather than delay the authorisation. Swissmedic accepted this proposal because the existing sterility and stability data gave no indication of an integrity failure. The authorisation was granted on 9 July 2025, subject to the condition described below.

Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals committed to submit container closure integrity test results for the 4 mL and 10 mL Calvidronate vials. The results should come from a validated deterministic method, such as vacuum decay or high-voltage leak detection. Testing should cover vials at release and vials stored at the labelled refrigerated condition up to at least the end of the approved shelf life. Where real-time data at end of shelf life are not yet available, please include a justification. The submission should also report the method validation, including the detection limit and the positive controls used. The test results are due on 31 August 2026. This condition remains open. We have not yet received any submission addressing it, and our records show no request to extend the deadline.

Marcus Ellery, Head of Stability Operations at Aldencourt, owns this commitment. We therefore address this letter to you directly and ask that you coordinate the response with your regulatory affairs colleagues.

We ask you to send the results as a variation application through the Swissmedic Portal, quoting the reference numbers shown at the top of this letter. If Aldencourt expects that it cannot meet the due date, please tell us in writing well before 31 August, giving the reasons and a revised timeline. Any extension is at Swissmedic's discretion. We would also be grateful for a short status update by 30 May 2026 confirming that testing is under way at the contract laboratory. If the results show any loss of integrity, please contact us without delay, whatever the reporting timelines. In that case, Aldencourt should also assess the potential impact on batches already on the Swiss market.

Please note that failing to fulfil a condition of authorisation may lead Swissmedic to take further measures under the Therapeutic Products Act. These may include changes to the authorisation.

For questions about this letter, please contact the case manager named below at the Authorisation Division, by telephone on +41 58 462 41 87 or through the Swissmedic Portal message function.

Yours sincerely,

Dr Annelies Brunner, Case Manager, Quality Assessment, Authorisation Division, Swissmedic

# FDA Reminder on an Overdue Commitment: Moxilurine 150 mg Hard Capsules (NDA 980619)

Department of Health and Human Services, Food and Drug Administration, Center for Drug Evaluation and Research, Office of Pharmaceutical Quality, 10903 New Hampshire Avenue, Silver Spring, MD 20993

June 2, 2026

NDA 980619. Agency Reference: OPQ-PMC-2026-04471. Postmarketing Commitment Tracking Number: 980619-PMC-3

Dr. Lena Vogt, Regulatory Affairs Manager, Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals, 2200 Harbor Point Drive, Suite 400, Wilmington, DE 19801

Dear Dr. Vogt:

This letter concerns your New Drug Application (NDA) 980619 for Moxilurine 150 mg hard capsules. The product consists of hydroxypropyl methylcellulose (HPMC) capsule shells and is marketed in PVC/PVDC blister packs and in 30-, 60- and 90-count high-density polyethylene (HDPE) bottles. The Agency is writing to remind Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals of a chemistry, manufacturing and controls commitment that has passed its agreed due date without the required submission.

During the review of the original application, the Agency issued an information request dated January 18, 2024, which noted that the long-term stability data supporting the proposed shelf life came mainly from the blister presentation and the 30-count bottle. The data for the 90-count HDPE bottle were limited to 6 months at the time. In that bottle configuration the headspace-to-fill ratio, the moisture ingress per dose and the number of repeated openings during patient use differ from the smaller bottles. The Agency therefore asked for confirmatory long-term data for this presentation. In a teleconference held on February 27, 2024 (Meeting ID 2024-T-0882), your representatives agreed to continue the ongoing long-term stability study on the 90-count HDPE bottle presentation through the 24-month time point and to submit the results to the application. This agreement was recorded as a postmarketing commitment in the approval letter for NDA 980619 dated March 22, 2024. The approval letter granted the proposed shelf life for all presentations on the condition that the confirmatory data would be submitted.

Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals committed to submit 24-month stability data for Moxilurine capsules packaged in 90-count HDPE bottles. The data were due on May 15, 2026. The Agency has reviewed the submissions received for NDA 980619 through the date of this letter. The most recent annual report, received on April 30, 2026 (Sequence 0031), contains an update on the study through the 18-month time point. However, the Agency has not received the 24-month results, a request to extend the due date, or an explanation for the delay. The Agency notes that this commitment is overdue and remains open.

<!-- page 2 -->

The 18-month results reported in the annual report do not, in themselves, raise a concern requiring immediate action. Even so, the 24-month data are needed to confirm that the approved shelf life is appropriate for the 90-count bottle. Of particular interest are assay, dissolution, water content and the specified degradation products under long-term storage and in-use conditions.

Within 30 calendar days of the date of this letter, please take one of the following actions. You may submit the complete 24-month stability data for the 90-count HDPE bottle presentation to NDA 980619, together with a statistical evaluation of the data against the approved specifications. Alternatively, if the data are not yet available, you may submit a written explanation of the reason for the delay with a revised timeline for submission that the Agency can review. If any result at any time point is out of specification or out of trend, please report it with your investigation findings and any proposed corrective actions. Please identify your submission clearly as a response to postmarketing commitment 980619-PMC-3. Note that the status of this commitment will be reflected in the Agency's postmarketing commitment reporting. Continued failure to meet it may lead the Agency to reconsider the expiration dating period approved for this presentation.

Dr. Lena Vogt, Regulatory Affairs Manager at Aldencourt, is the responsible contact for this commitment. The Agency will direct all further correspondence on this matter to Dr. Vogt.

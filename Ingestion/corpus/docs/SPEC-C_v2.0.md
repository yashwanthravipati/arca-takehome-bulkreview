# Drug Product Specification: Calvidronate Concentrate for Solution for Infusion 5 mg/mL

**Document number:** SPEC-C
**Version:** 2.0
**Status:** Current
**Effective date:** 2025-04-22
**Supersedes:** SPEC-C version 1.1
**Owner:** Quality Control, Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals, Harwell Brook Site

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This specification defines the tests, analytical procedures and acceptance criteria that Calvidronate Concentrate for Solution for Infusion 5 mg/mL (Product C) must meet. It applies at batch release and at every stability time point.

1.2 It is the controlling quality standard for this product. It must be used for the following activities:

- certification of commercial batches by the Qualified Person;
- disposition of clinical supply batches;
- evaluation of stability data generated under STAB-007, STAB-008 and STAB-009.

1.3 This version introduces separate release and shelf-life acceptance criteria for assay and the specified degradation product CVD-oxide. It also makes the sub-visible particle test a routine shelf-life test.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This specification applies to all batches of Calvidronate Concentrate for Solution for Infusion 5 mg/mL manufactured at the Harwell Brook sterile manufacturing facility (Building 4, Fill Line F2). It covers the product in all three registered presentations:

- 4 mL fill in 6R Type I clear glass vials;
- 10 mL fill in 10R Type I clear glass vials;
- 20 mL fill in 20R Type I clear glass vials.

All three presentations are closed with 20 mm fluoropolymer-coated bromobutyl stoppers and aluminium flip-off seals.

2.2 This specification does not apply to:

- the drug substance, calvidronate sodium, which is controlled under the drug substance specification held by the Chemical Development group;
- the diluted infusion solution prepared in 0.9% Sodium Chloride. Stability of the diluted solution is investigated under STAB-008 and is not a release requirement.

2.3 The approved storage condition is 2–8 °C, protected from light, in the outer carton. The approved shelf life is 36 months from the date of manufacture. Shelf-life acceptance criteria apply to all batches up to the expiry date when stored under these conditions.

2.4 Batches placed on stability, as described below, are tested against the shelf-life criteria in Section 4:

| Protocol | Condition | Batches |
|---|---|---|
| STAB-007 | Long-term 5 °C ± 3 °C (Chamber CH-R-03); accelerated 25 °C ± 2 °C / 60% RH ± 5% RH (Chamber CH-A-11) | Registration batches C23-0412, C23-0413 and C23-0419 |
| STAB-009 | Annual ongoing programme | One commercial batch per presentation per year |

## 3. Product Description and Composition

3.1 **Description.** Calvidronate Concentrate for Solution for Infusion 5 mg/mL is a clear, colourless, sterile, non-pyrogenic aqueous concentrate. It contains no antimicrobial preservative. It is intended for dilution in 0.9% Sodium Chloride before intravenous infusion. Each vial is for single use only.

3.4 **Manufacturing process.** The product is compounded under nitrogen. It is then sterilised by filtration through two 0.22 µm PVDF filters in series and aseptically filled under a nitrogen overlay. The headspace oxygen target at filling is NMT 2.0%, and this is controlled in-process under SOP-PRD-2214. Nitrogen protection is the main measure used to control formation of CVD-oxide.

4.3 **Reporting of impurities.**

- Report impurities at or above the reporting threshold of 0.05%.
- Express results as area percent relative to the calvidronate peak, after applying the relative response factors listed in AM-C-105.
- Report results to two decimal places when below 1.0%, and to one decimal place at or above 1.0%.
- Total impurities is the sum of all impurities at or above 0.05%.

<!-- page 4 -->

4.4 **Rounding.** Results are rounded according to SOP-QC-0310, *Rounding and Reporting of Analytical Results*, before they are compared with the acceptance criteria.

4.5 **Out-of-specification results.** Investigate any result outside a release or shelf-life criterion in accordance with SOP-QC-0402, *Investigation of Out-of-Specification Results*. In addition, assess any stability result that falls outside the release criterion but within the shelf-life criterion as an out-of-trend signal under SOP-QC-0405.

## 5. Justification of Specification

5.1 **General approach.** The specification was established in accordance with ICH Q6A. Degradation product limits are set in accordance with ICH Q3B(R2). The criteria take into account the following data:

- release results from 14 batches manufactured between 2022 and 2024 (four clinical, three registration and seven commercial batches);
- 36-month long-term data under STAB-007;
- 6-month accelerated data under STAB-007;
- the first annual commitment batches under STAB-009.

The shelf-life criteria form the basis of this specification. Calvidronate concentrate for solution for infusion 5 mg/mL must comply throughout its shelf life with assay 95.0–105.0% of label claim, CVD-oxide not more than 0.5%, total impurities not more than 2.0%, pH 6.8 to 7.6, and sub-visible particles not more than 6000 per container at 10 µm or greater and not more than 600 per container at 25 µm or greater. Where release criteria are tighter, they were derived from the observed stability trend so that a batch meeting the release criteria is expected to remain within the shelf-life criteria up to expiry.

5.2 **Assay.**

- Release results ranged from 98.6% to 101.4% of label claim, with a mean of 100.1%.
- Under STAB-007 long-term conditions, the mean loss over 36 months was 1.6%. The worst-case individual batch (C23-0419, 4 mL presentation) declined by 2.3%.
- The release range of 97.0–103.0% allows for this loss together with analytical variability (intermediate precision RSD 0.6%). Batches released at the lower release limit are therefore expected to remain above 95.0% at expiry.
- The shelf-life range of 95.0–105.0% is consistent with ICH Q6A expectations for parenteral solutions.

5.3 **CVD-oxide.** CVD-oxide is the N-oxide of calvidronate. It is the principal degradation product and forms by oxidation of the tertiary amine.

- At release, levels ranged from 0.04% to 0.12%.
- Under long-term storage, CVD-oxide increased by a maximum of 0.21% over 36 months.
- At accelerated conditions (25 °C/60% RH), levels reached 0.38% at 6 months. This confirms the temperature sensitivity of the product and supports refrigerated storage.
- The maximum daily dose is 100 mg calvidronate. At this dose, the ICH Q3B(R2) qualification threshold is 0.5% or 200 µg total daily intake, whichever is lower (0.2% at 100 mg).
- CVD-oxide has been qualified at levels up to 1.2% in the 13-week repeat-dose intravenous toxicity study in rats (study report TOX-CVD-0217), with no CVD-oxide-related findings.
- The shelf-life limit of NMT 0.5% is therefore qualified. The release limit of NMT 0.3% allows for the observed long-term increase.

<!-- page 5 -->

5.4 **CVD-desmethyl.** CVD-desmethyl is a process-related impurity carried over from the drug substance. It does not increase on storage.

- It was observed at 0.06–0.11% across all batches.
- It was qualified in the same toxicology study.
- The release limit of NMT 0.2% and the shelf-life limit of NMT 0.3% reflect batch history and allow for analytical variability.

5.5 **Unspecified impurities.**

- At the maximum daily dose of 100 mg, the ICH Q3B(R2) identification threshold is 0.2%. The shelf-life limit for any unspecified impurity is set at this value.
- The release limit of NMT 0.10% reflects manufacturing capability.
- No unspecified impurity above 0.08% has been observed in any batch.

5.6 **Total impurities.**

- At release, total impurities ranged from 0.21% to 0.44%.
- The highest 36-month long-term result was 0.78% (batch C23-0413, 20 mL).
- The shelf-life limit of NMT 2.0% provides a margin that accounts for the individual specified limits and possible minor late-forming degradants.
- The release limit of NMT 1.0% is consistent with process capability.

5.7 **pH.**

- The formulation is buffered with citrate to a target of pH 7.2.
- Calvidronate shows minimum degradation to CVD-oxide between pH 6.8 and 7.6 (pre-formulation report PF-CVD-009). Above pH 7.8, oxidation is accelerated.
- No pH drift greater than 0.1 unit was observed over 36 months.
- The same range is applied at release and shelf life, because the range itself is the stability-relevant control.

5.8 **Osmolality.**

- The target osmolality is 300 mOsmol/kg.
- The range of 270–330 mOsmol/kg ensures that the concentrate is not unduly hypertonic before dilution and supports compatibility with the infusion diluent.

5.9 **Sub-visible and visible particles.**

- The limits are those of Ph. Eur. 2.9.19 and USP <788> for preparations with a nominal content of 100 mL or less, applied per container.
- Sub-visible particle testing was added as a shelf-life test in this version. This change follows the observation, under STAB-007, of a modest increase in particles ≥10 µm in the 20 mL presentation at 24 months (maximum 410 per container).
- The increase was attributed to stopper-derived silicone droplets. It remains well within the limits, but routine monitoring through to expiry is considered appropriate.

5.10 **Bacterial endotoxins.**

- The endotoxin limit is calculated as K/M:
  - K is 5.0 EU/kg (intravenous route);
  - M is the maximum dose per kg per hour, 2.0 mg/kg, which corresponds to 100 mg for a 50 kg patient within a one-hour infusion.
- This gives a calculated limit of 2.5 EU/mg calvidronate.

<!-- page 6 -->

5.11 **Sterility and container closure integrity.**

- Sterility at release is a compendial requirement.
- During shelf life, sterility is confirmed at selected time points. Deterministic container closure integrity testing is performed at all other time points, in line with USP <1207>.

5.12 **Tests not included.** The following tests were considered and not included:

- **Colour (Ph. Eur. 2.2.2 instrumental):** no colour change has been observed in any batch, and the appearance test is sufficient.
- **Chiral purity:** calvidronate is achiral.
- **Antimicrobial preservative content:** the product contains no preservative.
- **Mannitol content:** mannitol content is controlled by the manufacturing process and verified through osmolality.

6.3 **System suitability.** System suitability requirements for AM-C-104 and AM-C-105 include the following:

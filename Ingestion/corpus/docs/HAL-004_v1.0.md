# Post-Authorisation Commitment: Moxilurine 150 mg Hard Capsules (PL 55821/0013)

Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency, 10 South Colonnade, Canary Wharf, London E14 4PU, United Kingdom

Date: 12 February 2026

Our reference: PL 55821/0013-0024/VAR. Commitment tracking reference: MHRA-PAC-2025-1187. Case officer reference: QLT/CHEM/0419.

Mr Thomas Okafor, Analytical Development Lead, Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals Ltd, Unit 4, Ashbridge Science Park, Cambridge CB4 0WS, United Kingdom

Dear Mr Okafor,

I am writing about the post-authorisation commitment made by Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals Ltd for Moxilurine 150 mg hard capsules, marketing authorisation PL 55821/0013. The product consists of 150 mg of active substance filled into hydroxypropyl methylcellulose (HPMC) capsule shells. It is presented in PVC/PVDC blisters and in high-density polyethylene bottles of 30, 60 and 90 capsules. This letter sets out the background to the commitment, its current status and what the Agency expects of the licence holder over the coming months.

On 14 March 2025 the licence holder submitted a Type II variation application (submission number MHRA-V2-2025-03318). The application sought approval of an alternate supplier of HPMC capsule shells, in addition to the currently approved shell manufacturer. The application included comparative physicochemical data for the two shell sources, batch analysis results for three registration-scale batches filled into the alternate shells, and supportive stability data under long-term and accelerated conditions. During assessment, the quality assessor noted that the dissolution method had been validated only with capsules made from the original supplier's shells. HPMC shells from different manufacturers can differ in gelling agent, wall thickness and disintegration behaviour. The assessor therefore questioned whether the method's discriminatory power, specificity and robustness had been shown for product filled into the alternate shells.

These points were discussed at a teleconference between the Agency and the licence holder on 22 July 2025 (meeting reference MHRA-TC-2025-0712). At that meeting the licence holder proposed to complete a full revalidation after approval rather than delay the variation. The Agency accepted the proposal because the batch dissolution results already submitted showed comparable release profiles from both shell sources. The variation was approved on 18 September 2025, and the commitment was recorded in the approval letter of that date.

The licence holder committed to revalidate the dissolution method for Moxilurine 150 mg capsules filled into HPMC shells from the alternate supplier. The revalidation is expected to cover, at a minimum, specificity against shell excipients, linearity and range, accuracy, precision and robustness. It should also show that the method can discriminate between acceptable and deliberately manufactured non-conforming batches in the alternate shells. The report should include all three pack presentations where the licence holder considers that packaging may influence shell behaviour during storage. The revalidation report is due on 30 June 2026. This commitment remains open. As of the date of this letter, the Agency has received no revalidation data and no interim update. We note, however, that the due date has not yet passed. Thomas Okafor, Analytical Development Lead at Aldencourt, owns this commitment. The Agency will direct any related correspondence to you.

<!-- page 2 -->

We ask that the licence holder submit the completed revalidation report, together with any consequent changes to the dissolution procedure described in the dossier, as a variation or as a commitment fulfilment submission, whichever is appropriate. Please quote the tracking reference given above. If the revalidation identifies any need to change the dissolution specification or method conditions, please contact us early so that the correct regulatory route can be agreed. If the licence holder expects that the due date cannot be met, please write to us no later than 30 April 2026. That request should explain the reasons for the delay and propose a revised timetable. The Agency would also welcome a short progress update by 31 March 2026, although this is not mandatory.

Questions about this letter should be addressed to the case officer, Dr Harriet Llewellyn, Chemistry, Quality and Pharmacy Unit, by email at quality.commitments@mhra.gov.uk or by telephone on 020 3080 6000.

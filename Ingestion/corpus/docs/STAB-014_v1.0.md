# Stability Protocol Supporting Manufacture of Zentoprazine Extended-Release Tablets at the Lindgren Site

**Document No.:** STAB-014 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective Date:** 2025-07-21 | **Status:** Current
**Product:** Product E, Zentoprazine Extended-Release Tablets, 50 mg, 100 mg and 200 mg, HDPE bottles
**Document Type:** Stability Protocol | **Owner:** Stability and Analytical Sciences, Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals

## 1. Purpose

This protocol defines the stability study for Zentoprazine Extended-Release Tablets manufactured at the Aldencourt Lindgren manufacturing site. The study is designed to generate data that show product made at Lindgren has stability equivalent to product made at the original commercial site, the Aldencourt Marston site. The data will support the post-approval site change submission.

Under the FDA guidance *SUPAC-MR: Modified Release Solid Oral Dosage Forms*, the transfer is classified as a Level 3 site change. Accordingly, this protocol specifies:

- the batches to be placed on study;
- the storage conditions and testing schedule;
- the analytical methods and acceptance criteria;
- the reduced-design justification; and
- the stability commitments that apply after approval.

## 2. Scope

This protocol applies to registration-equivalent batches of Zentoprazine Extended-Release Tablets 200 mg manufactured at the Lindgren site, Building L2, Oral Solids Suite 3. The batches use the commercial process described in Master Batch Record MBR-LG-0E-200, Revision 02. They are packaged in the commercial HDPE bottle configuration.

The protocol covers the following activities:

- sample placement, storage, pulling, testing, data trending and reporting;
- the stability commitments that follow approval of the site change.

The following are outside the scope of this protocol:

- Registration stability of the Marston-site product, which remains governed by STAB-013.
- The ongoing annual stability program for commercial batches, which is governed by STAB-015.
- Photostability. The packaged and unpackaged product was shown to be photostable under ICH Q1B Option 2 during registration studies under STAB-013. The site transfer introduces no change in formulation, primary packaging or tablet coating that would affect light sensitivity, so photostability testing is not repeated.

## 4. Materials and Batches

Three batches of Zentoprazine Extended-Release Tablets 200 mg will be manufactured at the Lindgren site. Each batch is produced at the full commercial batch size of 420,000 tablets. Each batch uses a different lot of zentoprazine drug substance and a different lot of the rate-controlling polymer, hypromellose K100M.

| Batch Number | Strength | Manufacture Date | Drug Substance Lot | Hypromellose K100M Lot | Batch Size (tablets) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LG25-E200-001 | 200 mg | 2025-06-02 | ZTP-DS-24117 | HK-3390218 | 420,000 |
| LG25-E200-002 | 200 mg | 2025-06-09 | ZTP-DS-24121 | HK-3390226 | 420,000 |
| LG25-E200-003 | 200 mg | 2025-06-16 | ZTP-DS-25004 | HK-3391002 | 420,000 |

All batches are packaged in the commercial container closure system:

- 75 cc white HDPE bottles, component code PKG-0E-B075;
- 38 mm polypropylene child-resistant closures with induction-seal liners, component code PKG-0E-C038;
- a 1 g silica gel desiccant canister in each bottle;
- 30 tablets per bottle.

This configuration is the same as that used for the Marston-site registration batches under STAB-013.

Each batch must be released by QA against SPEC-E before it is placed on stability. The Certificate of Analysis for each batch serves as the initial (time 0) data set, provided that testing is completed within 30 days of the end of packaging.

## 5. Analytical Methods

All testing is performed using validated methods. These methods were transferred from the Marston QC laboratory to the Lindgren QC laboratory under transfer protocol ATP-LG-2025-03. The transfer report was approved on 2025-05-14.

| Test | Method Number | Technique | Reportable Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Appearance | AM-GEN-005 | Visual examination | Conforms / does not conform |
| Assay | AM-ZTP-021 | Reversed-phase HPLC with UV detection at 254 nm | % of label claim |
| Related substances (ZTP N-oxide, ZTP desmethyl, unspecified impurities, total impurities) | AM-ZTP-022 | Gradient reversed-phase HPLC with UV detection at 230 nm | % area relative to zentoprazine |
| Drug release | AM-ZTP-024 | USP Apparatus 1 (baskets), 100 rpm, 900 mL pH 6.8 phosphate buffer, 37.0 ± 0.5°C; UV analysis | % released at 2, 8 and 16 hours |
| Water content | AM-ZTP-030 | Coulometric Karl Fischer | % w/w |
| Microbial enumeration | MIC-007 | Per USP <61> and <62> | CFU/g; absence of specified organisms |

All methods are stability-indicating for the attributes they measure. Specificity against degradation products was demonstrated through forced degradation studies under acid, base, oxidative, thermal and photolytic stress. Results are reported to the number of decimal places stated in SPEC-E. Impurity results below the reporting threshold of 0.05% are reported as "<0.05%".

## 6. Storage Conditions

Batches from the new manufacturing site will be stored at 25°C ± 2°C / 60% RH ± 5% RH and at 40°C ± 2°C / 75% RH ± 5% RH. These are the long-term and accelerated conditions defined in ICH Q1A(R2) for Climatic Zones I and II. They are the same conditions used for the registration batches under STAB-013.

The intermediate condition of 30°C ± 2°C / 65% RH ± 5% RH is not included in this study. If significant change, as defined in ICH Q1A(R2), occurs at the accelerated condition, the need for intermediate testing will be assessed under Section 9. Any intermediate testing will be added only by an approved protocol amendment.

| Condition | Chamber ID | Location | Qualification Report | Monitoring System |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Long-term | SC-LG-07 | Building L2, Room 2.114 | QR-SC-LG-07-2024 | Continuous EMS, 5-minute logging |
| Accelerated | SC-LG-09 | Building L2, Room 2.116 | QR-SC-LG-09-2024 | Continuous EMS, 5-minute logging |

The chambers are operated and maintained in accordance with SOP-FE-112 (Stability Chamber Operation and Monitoring). Any excursion outside the stated tolerances is handled as follows:

- an excursion lasting longer than 24 hours, or exceeding 2°C beyond the tolerance band for any duration, must be documented and assessed under Section 9;
- shorter excursions are recorded in the chamber log and reviewed by Stability Operations within one working day.

Samples are stored upright in their sealed commercial bottles. They are placed on chamber shelves designated in the placement map STAB-014-PM-01.

## 7. Testing Schedule

Testing will be conducted at 0, 3 and 6 months at both conditions, with long-term testing continuing at 9, 12, 18 and 24 months. The 0-month data are common to both conditions. Pull windows are as follows:

- ±7 days for time points up to and including 6 months;
- ±14 days for time points after 6 months.

Pull dates are calculated from the date of placement and are recorded in the stability management system (LIMS module STB).

## 8. Acceptance Criteria

The acceptance criteria are the shelf-life criteria in SPEC-E. Site-transfer batches must meet assay 95.0–105.0% of label claim, ZTP N-oxide not more than 0.3%, total impurities not more than 1.2%, and drug release of 15–35% at 2 hours, 50–75% at 8 hours and not less than 85% at 16 hours.

| Test | Acceptance Criterion |
|---|---|
| Appearance | Orange, oval, film-coated tablet debossed "AZ" on one side and "200" on the other |
| Assay | 95.0–105.0% of label claim |
| ZTP N-oxide | NMT 0.3% |
| ZTP desmethyl | NMT 0.5% |
| Any unspecified impurity | NMT 0.20% |
| Total impurities | NMT 1.2% |
| Drug release, 2 h | 15–35% |
| Drug release, 8 h | 50–75% |
| Drug release, 16 h | NLT 85% |
| Water content | NMT 4.0% w/w |
| Total aerobic microbial count | NMT 10³ CFU/g |
| Total yeasts and moulds count | NMT 10² CFU/g |
| *Escherichia coli* | Absent in 1 g |

Drug release is evaluated against the USP <711> acceptance table for extended-release dosage forms, Levels L1 to L3.

In addition to compliance with the acceptance criteria, Lindgren data will be compared with the Marston registration data from STAB-013. The comparison uses the following approaches:

<!-- page 5 -->

- Release profiles at each time point are compared using the similarity factor (f2). An f2 value of 50 or greater indicates similarity.
- Assay and ZTP N-oxide trends are evaluated by regression analysis with poolability testing at a significance level of 0.25, in accordance with ICH Q1E.

## 9. Deviation Handling

Any departure from this protocol must be documented and assessed under SOP-QA-031 (Deviation Management). Examples include:

- missed pull windows;
- chamber excursions beyond the limits in Section 6;
- testing outside the 30-day window;
- use of a method version other than the one approved;
- loss or damage of samples.

Deviations are classified as minor, major or critical. QA must approve each classification and impact assessment before the affected data are used in any report.

Out-of-specification results are investigated under SOP-QC-044 (Investigation of Out-of-Specification Results). Out-of-trend results are identified and investigated under SOP-QC-047 (Stability Trending and Out-of-Trend Evaluation). Results are considered out of trend when they fall outside the 95% prediction interval from the Marston registration data, or when they change by more than 1.0% absolute in assay between consecutive time points.

Significant change at the accelerated condition triggers a documented assessment by Stability and Analytical Sciences and Regulatory Affairs. That assessment must be completed within 15 working days.

Protocol amendments require the same approvals as this protocol. They are issued as a new version of STAB-014.

No deviations have been recorded under this protocol as of the effective date.

## 10. Bracketing and Matrixing

A bracketing design per ICH Q1D is applied to the strengths. Only the 200 mg strength is manufactured for this study because it has the highest drug load and the greatest potential for release-rate change, and the bracketing approach approved in STAB-013 covers the lower strengths. The bracketing is justified on the following grounds:

- All three strengths are made from a common blend.
- They differ only in tablet weight and tooling.
- They use identical excipient ratios.
- They are packaged in the same container closure system.

Under STAB-013, registration stability data for the 50 mg and 200 mg extremes showed comparable degradation and release-profile behaviour through 24 months. The 100 mg intermediate strength was bracketed at that time. The site transfer introduces no change to formulation, process parameters or packaging that would invalidate these grounds.

No matrixing of time points or batches is applied in this study. All batches are tested at every scheduled time point. The matrixing design for routine commercial batches is defined separately in STAB-015 and does not apply to the batches in Section 4.

## 11. Post-Approval Commitments

Approval of the Lindgren site will carry the following commitment. The first three commercial batches from the new site will be placed on long-term stability through 36 months, and a comparison with the original site data will be included in the next annual report.

These commercial batches are additional to the three batches in Section 4. They will be tested at 25°C ± 2°C / 60% RH ± 5% RH at 0, 3, 6, 9, 12, 18, 24 and 36 months. Testing will use the methods in Section 5 and the acceptance criteria in Section 8.

After the commitment batches complete the program, Lindgren commercial production will be enrolled in the annual program under STAB-015. Regulatory Affairs will track the commitment in the regulatory commitments register under entry RC-0E-2025-07.

# Annual Ongoing Stability Protocol for Commercial Calvidronate Concentrate

**Document number:** STAB-009
**Version:** 3.0
**Effective date:** 2025-02-17
**Status:** Current
**Product:** Product C, Calvidronate concentrate for solution for infusion 5 mg/mL (4 mL, 10 mL and 20 mL Type I glass vials)
**Document owner:** Regulatory Affairs, Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals
**Site of testing:** Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals, Quality Control Laboratory, Building 4, Harrowfield Site

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This protocol defines the annual ongoing (follow-up) stability program for commercial batches of Calvidronate concentrate for solution for infusion 5 mg/mL. The program confirms that commercial product continues to meet its approved shelf-life specification throughout the labelled shelf life of 36 months when stored refrigerated.

1.2 This program is distinct from the registration and long-term stability studies described in STAB-007 and from the in-use stability studies after dilution described in STAB-008. Its purpose is to monitor the stability of routine commercial production. It is not intended to establish or extend shelf life. Any proposal to change the shelf life shall be supported by a separate protocol and data evaluated in line with ICH Q1E.

1.3 The data generated under this protocol support the annual Product Quality Review (PQR) and the post-approval commitments made to the competent authorities.

## 2. Scope

2.1 This protocol applies to commercial batches of Product C manufactured at the Harrowfield Site and released for sale in any market in which Aldencourt Pharmaceuticals is the marketing authorisation holder.

2.2 This protocol covers the 4 mL, 10 mL and 20 mL presentations. Section 10 describes how these presentations are represented by the enrolled fill volumes.

2.3 This protocol does not apply to the following:

- development, clinical or registration batches, which are covered by STAB-007;
- diluted admixtures in 0.9% sodium chloride, which are covered by STAB-008;
- batches placed on stability as a consequence of a change control, process change or investigation, which require a study-specific protocol approved under SOP-QA-0412 (Change Control).

## 4. Materials and Batches

4.1 Each calendar year, the Stability Coordinator selects at least one commercial batch for enrolment from the batches released during that year. The selection is normally the first batch of the designated fill volume that is released in the year (see Section 10). Where a significant manufacturing change has occurred in the year, the first batch manufactured after the change should be selected in preference.

5.2 pH meters are calibrated before each use with certified buffers of pH 4.01, 7.00 and 10.01 per SOP-QC-0142. Analysts shall check that each buffer is within its expiry date and record the buffer lot numbers in the LIMS before testing. A calibration with an expired buffer invalidates any result obtained after it.

5.3 Any change to a method listed above shall follow change control. Before the revised method is used for stability testing, the change shall be assessed for comparability with historical stability data.

## 6. Storage Conditions

6.1 Commercial batches enrolled in the ongoing program are stored at 5°C ± 3°C in the inverted orientation, which is the worst case for stopper interaction. The vials are placed on the shelves stopper down so that the solution is in continuous contact with the stopper surface. This maximises the potential for extractables and for sorption of calvidronate to the elastomer.

6.2 The primary storage location is stability refrigerator SC-12 (Building 4, Room 4.117). It is qualified per SOP-FE-0120, mapped annually, and monitored at 5-minute intervals by two independent CMS probes. The alarm limits are 2.5°C and 7.5°C (pre-alarm) and 2.0°C and 8.0°C (action alarm). Stability refrigerator SC-14 in the same room is the qualified backup location.

6.3 No other storage conditions are part of this protocol. The accelerated (25°C ± 2°C / 60% RH ± 5% RH) and stress conditions used for registration batches are defined only in STAB-007, and the diluted-solution conditions are defined only in STAB-008.

6.4 Samples removed for testing are kept at 2–8°C in validated transport boxes and tested within 5 working days of the pull. If testing cannot be completed in that time, the QC Laboratory Manager shall be informed.

## 7. Testing Schedule

7.1 Annual batches are tested at 0, 6, 12, 24 and 36 months. The T0 result is the batch release result, provided that release testing was completed within 30 days of the T0 placement date. Otherwise, a separate T0 test is performed on the stability samples.

7.2 The pull windows are ±7 days at the 6-month time point and ±14 days at the 12-, 24- and 36-month time points, measured from the T0 placement date. A pull outside the window is a deviation and shall be handled per Section 9.

7.3 Table 2 lists the tests performed at each time point and the number of vials required for the 4 mL fill. For the 20 mL fill, the vial numbers for sub-visible particles and extractable volume are reduced as stated in the footnote.

## 8. Acceptance Criteria

8.1 Results are assessed against the current specification limits of assay 95.0–105.0% of label claim, CVD-oxide not more than 0.5%, total impurities not more than 2.0%, and pH 6.8 to 7.6. These limits are the shelf-life limits in SPEC-C. If SPEC-C is revised, the limits in force at the date of testing apply, and the change shall be recorded in the annual stability report.

8.2 The other attributes are assessed against SPEC-C. A summary is given in Table 3 for convenience; in case of any discrepancy, SPEC-C prevails.

**Table 3. Shelf-life acceptance criteria (summary of SPEC-C)**

| Attribute | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|
| Appearance | Clear, colourless to slightly yellow solution |
| Colour | Not more intensely coloured than reference solution Y6 |
| Assay | 95.0–105.0% of label claim |
| CVD-oxide | NMT 0.5% |
| Any unspecified impurity | NMT 0.20% |
| Total impurities | NMT 2.0% |
| pH | 6.8–7.6 |
| Visible particles | Practically free from visible particles |
| Sub-visible particles (≥10 µm / ≥25 µm) | NMT 6000 / NMT 600 per container |
| Extractable volume | Not less than nominal volume |
| Bacterial endotoxins | NMT 1.5 EU/mg calvidronate |
| Sterility | Sterile |
| Container closure integrity | No leak detected |

8.3 Note: the release limit for assay in SPEC-C (97.0–103.0% of label claim) is tighter than the shelf-life limit and is not applied to stability time points.

8.4 Out-of-trend (OOT) evaluation is performed per SOP-QC-0231. Each new result for assay and CVD-oxide is compared with the batch's own regression and with the historical commercial data set. A result outside the 95% prediction interval is flagged as OOT and evaluated by QC and QA.

## 9. Deviation Handling

9.1 Any departure from this protocol is recorded and investigated per SOP-QA-0301 (Deviation Management). Examples include chamber excursions, missed or late pulls, invalidated results and sample handling errors. Confirmed out-of-specification results are investigated per SOP-QC-0230. Regulatory Affairs shall assess whether the competent authorities must be notified, normally within the timelines defined in SOP-RA-0050.

9.2 For a temperature excursion, QA shall perform an assessment using the mean kinetic temperature and the known degradation kinetics of calvidronate from the registration studies in STAB-007. The assessment shall conclude either that the stored samples remain representative or that the affected batches must be withdrawn from the program and replaced.

9.3 The following deviations occurred during the period covered by this revision and are documented here for traceability.

Deviation DEV-2025-0034 documented that stability refrigerator SC-12 reached 9.4°C for approximately 3 hours during a site power interruption, and a temperature excursion assessment found no impact on the stored batches. The CMS action alarm activated, and the refrigerator returned to the 2–8°C range once power was restored. The samples were not moved to SC-14 because restoration was expected before relocation could be completed. The calculated mean kinetic temperature for the monitoring period remained within 2–8°C, and registration data showed no measurable change in assay or CVD-oxide after 3 months at 25°C. As a corrective action, Facilities and Engineering connected SC-12 to the uninterruptible backup supply (CAPA-2025-0011).

Under deviation DEV-2025-0102, the 12-month pH result for batch CV24C011 was invalidated because an expired calibration buffer was used, and the sample was retested within the pull window. The retest used reserve vials stored under the protocol condition and a pH meter calibrated with in-date buffers. The retest result was within 6.8–7.6 and consistent with the batch's earlier results. The original result was retained in the LIMS and annotated as invalid. CAPA-2025-0019 added a LIMS check that prevents a pH result being entered unless in-date buffer lots are recorded (see Section 5.2).

9.4 Deviations are summarised in the annual stability report, with an assessment of their impact on the data set.

## 10. Bracketing and Matrixing

10.1 In line with the registration bracketing design, the 4 mL and 20 mL fill volumes are enrolled in alternate years and the 10 mL fill is not separately enrolled. The 4 mL fill has the highest stopper-surface-to-volume ratio, and the 20 mL fill has the largest headspace and longest fill duration. Together they bracket the 10 mL fill. All three presentations use the same formulation, glass type and stopper composition and are made by the same process. This approach was accepted at registration on the basis of ICH Q1D. It is supported by STAB-007 data showing comparable stability across all three fill volumes.

10.2 Enrolment alternates as follows: 4 mL in even-numbered years and 20 mL in odd-numbered years, starting with the 2022 enrolment. Table 1 reflects this sequence.

10.3 No matrixing of time points or batches is applied under this protocol. Every enrolled batch is tested at every time point for the attributes shown in Table 2.

10.4 If ongoing data show a difference between the 4 mL and 20 mL fills that could call the bracketing assumption into question, QA and Regulatory Affairs shall evaluate whether the 10 mL fill must be enrolled. Examples include a statistically significant difference in CVD-oxide slope or an OOT result in one fill only. Any change to the enrolment design requires revision of this protocol.

## 11. Post-Approval Commitments

11.1 The marketing authorisation holder commits to enrolling at least one commercial batch per year into this ongoing stability program for as long as the product is marketed. This commitment appears in Module 3.2.P.8.2 of the marketing authorisation dossier. It is tracked in the Regulatory Commitments Register under entry RC-C-003.

11.2 In any year in which no commercial batch of the designated fill volume is manufactured, the Stability Coordinator shall enrol a batch of the alternate bracketing fill volume instead. The substitution shall be recorded in Table 1 in the next revision of this protocol. If no commercial batch at all is manufactured in a calendar year, QA shall document the gap, and Regulatory Affairs shall assess whether the authorities must be notified.

11.3 Results are reported in the PQR each year and are available for inspection. The final 36-month report for each batch is approved by QA within 60 days of completion of testing.
